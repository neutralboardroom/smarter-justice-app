# Smarter Justice v1.7.45 Release Notes

## Portal Presence Management and Acceptance

Attorneys and authorized firms can now maintain a separate private profile section for Divorce Law Aid, Estate Law Aid, and Personal Injury Law Aid from the central Smarter Justice dashboard without creating a portal account or paying to control basic profile information. Writes use expected revisions; submissions and owner approvals apply to an exact portal revision and exact central source revision.

Approved portal-specific data is added only to the existing read-only, allowlisted professional handoff. Stale central edits withhold the portal-specific projection until it is reviewed again.

The private owner Control Center now includes fail-closed acceptance records for canonical attorney and firm pages, public search, firm-attorney links, import receipts, suppression, last-known-good state, and rollback. These records distinguish local preparation, staging acceptance, and production activation. Production activation remains disabled.

Personal Injury portal-specific matter types include vehicle and general personal-injury paths while Workers’ Compensation and Medical Malpractice remain separate.

## Status

Not deployed. Live portal imports, automatic writes, paid products, sponsored placement, opportunities, and all other commercial/public activation gates remain closed.
