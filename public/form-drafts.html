Selected Form Support

Form drafts are prepared carefully, one supported path at a time.

For selected paths, users can add missing details and receive a worksheet or draft for review. These are not filed forms. Before a draft is provided, the official instructions, required answers, review needs, and the user’s own information must be confirmed.

Current safeguards

  • Users can add missing details from the dashboard.
  • Reviewers can correct draft details without changing the user’s original answers.
  • Missing information and supporting documents must be addressed before a draft is marked ready.
  • Required details, supporting documents, and review notes are considered together.

Safety rules

  • A qualified review is required before a draft is provided when the selected path calls for it.
  • Official instructions, filing method, fees, signature requirements, and the current form version must be checked.
  • Missing required fields block delivery.
  • Attorney, tax attorney, CPA, enrolled agent, accountant, benefits professional, or injury attorney review may be recommended.
  • Smarter Justice does not guarantee approval, acceptance, settlement, benefits, timing, refund, or legal/tax outcome.
