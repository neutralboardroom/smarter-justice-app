What is not active

Clear limits protect users from false promises.

Confidential uploads

Private server storage and confidential document upload are not open. The current document workspace is device-only.

Paid Human Review

Public paid Human Review and professional review purchasing remain paused until staffing, security, payment, support, and legal gates pass.

Automatic filing or routing

Smarter Justice does not automatically file forms, contact a professional, create a commercial lead, or guarantee that a professional will accept a matter.

Important

Smarter Justice is not a law firm, court, government agency, insurer, or tax authority. Free tools provide organization and general starting information, not individualized professional advice or guaranteed outcomes.
