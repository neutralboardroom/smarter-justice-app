What this check can and cannot do

  • It can identify visible organization gaps and patterns using deterministic rules.
  • It can point to the section or line that triggered a finding.
  • It cannot determine truth, credibility, authenticity, admissibility, legal merit, evidence sufficiency, case strength, or which source controls.
  • It cannot calculate a legal deadline or decide whether a filing is complete.
  • A warning can be a false positive. Review the original item before changing anything.
