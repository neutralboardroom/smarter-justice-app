Evidence still required before paid professional enrollment opens.

This no-index owner page summarizes launch boundaries without displaying secret values. A feature foundation is not the same as tested production readiness.

Required before accepting paid founding members

  • Production-appropriate database, persistent account records, backup, and successful restore test
  • Confirmed owner account persistence, authenticator MFA, recovery codes, and removal of temporary bootstrap credentials
  • Authenticated transactional email with tested verification, reset, invitation, billing, and security messages
  • Complete payment test lifecycle, signed-event verification, duplicate protection, cancellation, refund, and failed-payment handling
  • Awaited transactional writes and idempotency for claims, memberships, firms, seats, approvals, and billing changes
  • Professional membership terms, credential procedures, advertising and ethics review, complaints, support, and escalation procedures
  • Real-device professional signup, dashboard, profile, firm, seat, billing, and recovery acceptance
  • Named first cohort, capacity limit, support ownership, incident process, pause control, and explicit owner approval

Deliberately unavailable until separate proof exists

  • Confidential public document upload and saved-file creation
  • Unrestricted professional matching or opportunity routing
  • Public reviews and ratings
  • Public consultation booking
  • Any claim of guaranteed clients, matters, revenue, eligibility, approvals, or outcomes
Current safe scope:

The professional-first public experience, profile and firm foundations, non-saved public routing preview, and controlled membership preparation can be inspected. Paid activation remains owner-controlled and must stay paused until the evidence above is complete.

Private owner workflow. Configuration and code foundations do not replace operational evidence. Smarter Justice is not a law firm, tax firm, accounting firm, or government agency.
