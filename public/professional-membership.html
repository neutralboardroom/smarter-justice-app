Professional FAQ

Questions before creating an account

Does a paid product guarantee leads or clients?

No. Paid products may provide clearly labeled Sponsored visibility or eligibility for professional opportunities after all gates are satisfied. Smarter Justice does not guarantee inquiries, matters, clients, revenue, ranking, or results.

Do I have to pay to claim or edit my basic profile?

No. Basic profile claiming, verification, correction, and editing are free after identity and authority review. Payment is required only for optional paid products and never makes a profile verified, specialty-eligible, endorsed, or organically preferred.

Who may create an account and later apply?

The initial launch is designed for attorneys and law firms whose identity, authority, credentials, and portal relevance can be reviewed.

How does recurring billing work?

The approved pilot cadence is monthly only. The individual profile is $15 per month. A claimed firm profile is $15 per month plus $15 per covered attorney. Checkout remains closed until the separate payment, support, legal, and owner gates pass; renewal and cancellation information must be presented before payment.

Can a firm manage multiple professionals?

Yes. Approved firm administrators can manage the firm workspace, covered seats, offices, and professional records, subject to permissions and individual credential requirements.

What happens if enrollment is temporarily unavailable?

You may create an account, locate or request a profile, and prepare application information. Payment remains unavailable until enrollment is open and the application is approved. You will not be charged merely for applying.

Paid growth is optional—not a promise of clients or results.

Payment does not improve credential status, specialty eligibility, organic order, substantive guidance, or professional suitability. Identity, credentials, service scope, jurisdiction, agreements, availability, and platform approval are reviewed separately.
