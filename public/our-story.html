Our story

Built from street-level experience—not from a conference room alone.

Smarter Justice grew from years of street-level outreach through Justice Truck—meeting people where they were, listening to their legal concerns, speaking with attorneys, and seeing how difficult it could be to find the right starting point.

What the outreach revealed

People often needed a clearer first step.

Many people did not know what type of legal, tax, benefits, business, or government-form help they needed. They might have had a notice, deadline, dispute, injury, family problem, tax concern, or business obligation without knowing which category, portal, or professional was appropriate.

At the same time, attorneys and other professionals needed better ways to present accurate information about their services and connect with people whose needs actually matched their experience.

What changed

Technology made a broader starting point possible.

Smarter Justice uses focused portals, rules-based starting tools, and optional AI-assisted organization to help people describe a problem in ordinary language, understand likely next steps, and see when human or professional help may be appropriate.

Technology does not replace the judgment, responsibility, or relationship of a qualified attorney, tax professional, or other licensed professional. It can, however, help people arrive better organized and better informed.

How the connected network helps

Street-level public service

The Stop Sign Project made resource information visible where someone might find it privately.

Justice Truck’s community work included the Stop Sign Project: durable domestic-violence resource signs intended for locations such as women’s restrooms in bars, restaurants, and public spaces. The signs display the National Domestic Violence Hotline and identify StopSignProject.org. The dedicated website is being prepared and will be linked here only after its destination and public resources are verified.

This work reflects the same principle behind Smarter Justice: meet people where they are, provide a clearer starting point, and connect them with appropriate resources without pretending that navigation is legal advice, emergency response, or professional representation.

Justice Truck and Smarter Justice

One history. Two connected public-access brands.

Justice Truck remains the community-access, vehicle, street, kiosk, event, and outreach brand. Smarter Justice is the broader network of focused portals, shared technology, professional profiles, and online starting tools. Each brand strengthens the other without erasing its separate role.
