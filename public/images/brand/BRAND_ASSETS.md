# Smarter Justice brand assets

Release family: v1.7.20

## Approved master identity

The existing Smarter Justice shield-and-forward-path identity is preserved. v1.7.20 does not replace or materially redesign the approved mark. It adds optimized technical variants so the same identity renders consistently in headers, browser tabs, mobile shortcuts, application metadata, and social sharing.

## Files and intended use

- `smarter-justice-logo-primary.svg` — scalable primary horizontal logo on light backgrounds.
- `smarter-justice-logo-primary.png` — transparent raster fallback for systems that do not accept SVG.
- `smarter-justice-mark.svg` — scalable compact shield mark.
- `smarter-justice-mark-512.png` — high-resolution compact raster mark.
- `favicon-32.png` and `favicon-48.png` — browser-icon fallbacks.
- `apple-touch-icon.png` — 180 × 180 Apple touch icon.
- `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` — web-app manifest icons.
- `smarter-justice-social.png` — 1200 × 630 social-sharing image.

## Provenance and restrictions

All variants are derived from the approved repository assets `/public/logo.svg` and `/public/favicon.svg`. No third-party logo, seal, stock mark, government emblem, law-firm branding, or copied artwork is used. The shield gradient is retained only inside the approved logo; ordinary public pages use the restrained classic light visual system.
