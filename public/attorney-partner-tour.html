Smarter Justice’s role

Central management without replacing specialty portals.

Smarter Justice coordinates professional accounts, one core profile, firm and office relationships, claims, verification workflows, membership, support, and approved read-only profile distribution. Each legal micro-portal remains a separate product with its own specialty content, public profile pages, tools, repository, deployment, and rollback.

  • One central professional identity and core profile.
  • Free basic profile claiming, correction, suppression, and removal.
  • Separate specialty evidence, portal eligibility, publication, and membership states.
  • No automatic two-way portal synchronization during the controlled launch.
