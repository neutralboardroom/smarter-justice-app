What this page does not do

  • It does not save a survivor narrative or legal search.
  • It does not collect a safe-contact choice or send automatic email or SMS.
  • It does not request precise location or disclose confidential shelter or service locations.
  • It does not contact police, courts, shelters, employers, relatives, alleged abusers, or other third parties.
  • It does not predict danger, rank survivors, guarantee safety, confirm capacity, or promise representation or outcomes.
