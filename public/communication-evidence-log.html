Keep the record useful

  • Identify whether an entry is a document, communication, observation, or preservation step.
  • Record the source, file name, page, message date, or contact route when available.
  • Distinguish exact quotations from your own summary.
  • Keep original files and metadata when safe; this log does not preserve evidence for you.
  • Verify urgent instructions and dates through the original source or an official channel.
This is not a recording tool

It does not record calls, capture messages, collect files, authenticate evidence, establish admissibility, notify anyone, or calculate legal deadlines.
