One roof

Growth, operations, and compliance should reinforce one another.

A growth system is more useful when the firm can act on the inquiry. An operating system is safer when permissions, consent, provenance, and handoffs are built in. Marketing is more durable when compliance review happens before content goes out—not after a complaint.

Growth

Help the right people discover and understand your firm.

  • Professional and firm presence across relevant legal areas.
  • Public tools that help prospective clients arrive better prepared.
  • Source and campaign attribution without requiring a private matter narrative.
  • Referral and growth-partner foundations with privacy boundaries.
  • Future content, reputation, outreach, and campaign tools only when their gates are satisfied.

Operations

Turn interest into an organized, controlled workflow.

  • Professional, firm, office, seat, and role controls.
  • Native-first contact, inquiry, intake, appointment, task, communication, campaign, and handoff objects.
  • Consent and suppression carried with the record instead of bolted on later.
  • Clear source/provenance and explicit merge receipts.
  • Native, hybrid, and external-primary adoption paths so firms are not forced into an all-or-nothing migration.

Compliance

Put jurisdiction-aware checks in front of marketing publication.

  • Check claims and potentially misleading statements.
  • Check testimonials, endorsements, specialist claims, and required responsible-lawyer or firm identification.
  • Consider solicitation method/channel and jurisdiction-specific restrictions.
  • Track sourced, versioned rules and the evidence used for a decision.
  • Explain flags and require human review or block publication when the system cannot determine compliance confidently.

How the pieces connect

From discovery to engagement without losing the thread.

1. DiscoverA person starts with a problem, legal area, free tool, profile, community resource, or referral path.
2. PreparePublic tools help organize facts, dates, questions, and documents before professional contact.
3. Inquire and intakeWhen enabled, structured inquiry and intake can enter the firm workflow with source and consent context.
4. OperateContacts, appointments, tasks, communications, firm roles, and handoffs can stay connected instead of being re-keyed across unrelated tools.
5. Market responsiblyGrowth content and campaigns can pass through jurisdiction-aware review before publication, with reasons and evidence preserved.
6. LearnPrivacy-minimized growth events can show which channels and workflows perform without selling prospective-client or client facts as marketing data.

  • Any claim that Smarter Justice guarantees legal compliance, leads, clients, or outcomes.
