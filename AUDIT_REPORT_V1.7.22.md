# Smarter Justice v1.7.22 Audit Report

## Release focus
v1.7.22 advances the approved cross-sector architecture from a static specification into a protected operating workspace and creates a clean standalone **Smarter Ecosystem Control Center v0.1.0** starter artifact. It also resumes the required source-tracked professional profile growth cycle with Northern Metro New Jersey as the lead market.

## Material improvements
- Added a protected, editable, metadata-only workspace for Smarter Justice, Smarter Health, Smarter Property, and Smarter Money.
- Seeded exact artifact names, versions, SHA-256 values, sizes, priorities, staff leads, next actions, and activation-gate status for the four sector umbrellas.
- Kept exact artifact identity immutable while allowing owner/staff updates to non-confidential operating metadata.
- Added portable migration/export records for a future standalone control center.
- Built and exact-tested the separate dependency-free `smarter-ecosystem-control-center-v0.1.0.zip` starter repository with private bearer-token owner/staff access, sector records, metadata updates, closed activation gates, export, and audit history.
- Added 12 source-tracked individual professionals, 2 firms, and 12 professional-firm links: Northern Metro New Jersey 8, Manhattan 2, Brooklyn 2.
- Increased the public directory to 65 professionals, 24 firms, and 89 total records, with 86 strict qualifying profiles.

## Data and authority boundaries
The cross-sector workspace and standalone starter coordinate portfolio, artifact, release, domain, deployment, priority, staffing, readiness, and gate metadata only. They do not centralize medical records, legal intake, financial records, property-security information, credentials, account data, or other confidential public-user records. Each sector's latest dedicated authoritative ZIP remains controlling for that sector.

## Safety conclusion
No payment, booking, upload, review, routing, filing, professional eligibility, sensitive-data, or broad cross-sector sharing gate was opened. The standalone control center is private, metadata-only, exact-artifact-tested, and not deployed. Production remains last verified at v1.6.1.
