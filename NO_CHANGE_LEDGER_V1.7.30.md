# Smarter Justice v1.7.30 No-Change Ledger

The following were deliberately preserved because no safer verified activation or replacement was available:

- Classic white visual system, approved logo, favicon, navigation, and established page architecture.
- Node 22.x major-version requirement; deployment should use a current supported Node 22 patch after acceptance.
- PostgreSQL and Nodemailer dependency architecture; no dependency was changed while the configured registry was unavailable.
- Server-enforced rejection of sensitive public submissions while sensitive traffic is unapproved.
- Closed confidential uploads, public/professional payments, paid Human Review Specialist services, booking, reviews, unrestricted routing, external AI for sensitive content, automatic filing, and broad cross-portal user-data sharing.
- Separation of listing, claim, identity, credential, firm authority, membership, participation, sponsorship, availability, inquiry eligibility, engagement, and outcome states.
- Neutral organic directory ordering and no copied headshots, reviews, ratings, awards, verdicts, settlements, or outcome claims.
- Dedicated legal portals remain authoritative for their own implementation; no automatic cross-repository synchronization was introduced.
