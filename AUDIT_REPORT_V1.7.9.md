# Smarter Justice v1.7.9 Audit Report

Prepared: July 21, 2026
Baseline: exact-tested `smarter-justice-v1.7.8.zip`
Rollback: `smarter-justice-v1.7.8.zip`
Deployment: not deployed; production remains last verified v1.6.1

## Evidence-based findings addressed

1. The approved Next Version Improvement List existed outside the release package but was not yet machine-readable, status-controlled, or visible in the owner Control Center.
2. The historical build master mixed completed history with current work.
3. Readiness could be interpreted too broadly without separate evidence dimensions.
4. Divorce, Criminal, and Employment specialty portals had current builds but the shared portal catalog still retained older generic practice ownership.
5. Known specialty-portal exact-build truth was not consistently reconciled in the portfolio seed.
6. The shared platform lacked a consolidated portal capability/deviation matrix.
7. The official-form catalog and guided workflows lacked one consolidated filing-boundary inventory.
8. Professional applicants needed a clearer explanation of verification, review, missing information, enrollment, and payment separation.
9. Profile source, claim, correction, removal, dispute, sponsorship, and audit operations required one explicit shared standard.

## Improvements completed

- Structured improvement list and active queue.
- Release evidence and immutable history.
- Fifteen readiness dimensions.
- Truthful specialty-portal catalog and portfolio records.
- Portal capability/deviation matrix.
- Form and document workflow inventory.
- Profile-data operations standard.
- Protected owner evidence views.
- Professional application expectation copy.
- Targeted regression coverage.

## Deliberately not changed

No paid or sensitive gate was opened. Public information architecture, pricing concepts, core customer funnel, security controls, forms themselves, professional ranking rules, and deployment infrastructure were not changed without evidence of a material benefit. External acceptance and production evidence remain on the maintained list.
