# Smarter Justice Shared Revenue and Access Standard

Version: 1.0.0  
Established: July 20, 2026

## Purpose

Every Smarter Justice portal must preserve a common access and revenue model while adapting scope, pricing, licensing, staffing, and professional boundaries to its specialty.

## Core model

1. Meaningful guided public starting help, with optional AI assistance only after an affirmative user choice remains free or extremely low cost.
2. Optional Human Review Specialist services are separately scoped, priced, staffed, and disclosed.
3. Attorneys, tax professionals, accountants, and other qualified professionals provide separately engaged professional services under clear independence and fee boundaries.
4. Attorneys, tax professionals, law firms, and accounting firms may pay low introductory recurring membership fees for approved platform participation.
5. A future optional public supporter or continuity membership, likely around $5–$10 monthly, may add higher fair-use limits, saved progress, continuity, and convenience without removing meaningful free access.
6. No plan is called unlimited unless real capacity, safeguards, and terms support that claim.

## Fee separation

Every public price display and checkout must distinguish:

- AI-guided platform access.
- Optional public supporter or continuity membership.
- Human Review Specialist services.
- Separately engaged attorney, tax, accounting, insurance, or other professional services.
- Government, court, filing, tax, premium, and agency fees.
- Third-party expenses.

## Human Review Specialist boundary

Human Review Specialists may organize information, check for requested items, identify obvious blanks, prepare questions, and help users understand the platform workflow. They may not be presented as licensed professionals or as providing legal, tax, accounting, insurance, or other regulated advice. Every separately priced service, checkout, order, fulfillment, cancellation, and refund workflow must also comply with `PUBLIC_PAID_HUMAN_REVIEW_STANDARD.md`.

## Professional membership boundary

Professional membership does not create credential verification, substantive eligibility, endorsement, ranking, guaranteed clients, guaranteed matters, guaranteed revenue, professional engagement, or outcomes.

## Public plan activation

Future public plans remain inactive until all of the following are evidenced:

- Secure persistent accounts and saved progress.
- Billing, renewal, cancellation, refund, and failed-payment operations.
- Fair-use, abuse, and commercial-volume controls.
- Support capacity.
- Privacy, security, retention, and deletion procedures.
- Accurate public terms and pricing.
- Explicit owner approval.

## Portal adoption

Every portal-specific build list and continuation prompt must state whether this standard is adopted, adapted, planned, deferred, or not applicable and explain any specialty-specific deviation.


## Free professional profile control and optional paid growth

- Attorneys and firms may claim, verify, correct, and edit approved basic profiles without payment.
- Optional paid products may add clearly labeled Sponsored visibility, enhanced prominence, lead eligibility, and access to case or attorney-review opportunities only after all independent gates are satisfied.
- Paid products do not purchase identity or credential verification, specialty eligibility, organic ranking, endorsement, reviews, or guaranteed work.
- Smarter Justice uses disclosed fixed subscription, advertising, platform, software, or opportunity-access charges. It does not take a percentage of legal fees, settlements, recoveries, refunds, or outcomes and does not make charges contingent on case results.
- Paid growth remains closed until attorney-advertising, solicitation, referral, fee-sharing, privacy, conflicts, billing, support, qualified-counsel, and owner approvals are recorded.
