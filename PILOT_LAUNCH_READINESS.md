## Stop Sign Project boundary

StopSignProject.org is confirmed owned and registered as the separate dedicated Domestic Violence Safety & Legal Help portal. Its complete legal, incident, evidence, document, court, safety, language, advocate, and professional workflows belong to a separately versioned build. Smarter Justice v1.7.7 does not claim that portal is complete, deployed, or live and does not accept domestic-violence confidential matter data through the general workflow.

Do not fabricate evidence or mark an external item complete merely to open a gate.
