3. The portal sends only minimum safe context to Smarter Justice:
   - Canonical portal ID
   - Local portal profile ID
   - Smarter Justice professional ID when known
   - Intended action
   - Allowlisted return destination
   - Signed or tamper-resistant state where appropriate
4. Smarter Justice handles account creation or sign-in.
5. Smarter Justice handles email verification.
6. Smarter Justice handles claim evidence and staff review.
7. Smarter Justice grants central management permission only after approval.
8. Smarter Justice handles firm, office, seat, billing, support, correction, suppression, and removal.
9. Smarter Justice creates an approved portal-specific projection.
10. The micro-portal imports and displays the approved projection.
11. “Manage profile” always returns the professional to Smarter Justice.

- A micro-portal may offer “Create a professional profile.”
