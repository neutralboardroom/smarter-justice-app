# Cross-Portal Learning Packet — v1.7.52

## Recommended reusable pattern

A portfolio-level demonstration should use one short central story, practice-specific sequencing, unmistakably synthetic data, safe tool handoffs, a stable return path, truthful unavailable-state fallback, and no claim that another repository adopted the pattern.

Adoption is **RECOMMENDED** for Divorce Law Aid and Estate Law Aid first, then Personal Injury Law Aid. Domestic Violence Aid requires its stricter survivor-safety overlay and must not inherit generic tracking, narrative collection or location behavior. Each portal must implement and exact-test its own visual/profile/tool changes.
