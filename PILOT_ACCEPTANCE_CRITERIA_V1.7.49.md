# Three-Portal Pilot Acceptance Criteria — v1.7.49

## Applies to Divorce, Estate, and Personal Injury

1. Independently verify the exact dedicated portal ZIP in its own builder chat.
2. Preserve one central Smarter Justice account, claim, verification, firm, seat, membership, billing, support, and core-profile authority.
3. Add no duplicate portal password, subscription, firm account, central editor, or central verification system.
4. Validate v1.4.0 destination, digest, fingerprints, revisions, publication approval, suppression, and prohibited-data boundary.
5. Support deterministic and idempotent private/public/suppress actions, rejection reports, conflicts, receipts, last-known-good preservation, and rollback.
6. Pass tamper, wrong-destination, replay, duplicate, stale-revision, private-record, payment-separation, and failed-import tests.
7. Pass mobile, keyboard, zoom, screen-reader, loading, empty, error, success, and confirmation acceptance.
8. Keep live imports, automatic writes, payments, inquiries, appointments, reviews, sponsorship, uploads, AI, and deployment closed until separately approved.
9. Produce one clean exact-tested portal ZIP and an evidence-based Smarter Justice handoff.

## Personal Injury additions

Vehicle accidents are included. Workers’ compensation remains separate. The Car Accident Law Aid repository is preserved but not separately launched. No liability, claim-value, serious-injury, deadline, settlement, or attorney-acceptance prediction is permitted.
