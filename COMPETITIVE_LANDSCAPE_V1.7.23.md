# Smarter Justice Competitive Landscape — v1.7.23

Research date: 2026-07-22

Owner-only product strategy evidence. This is not a legal conclusion, endorsement, market-share claim, or permission to copy competitor branding or content.

## Competitor patterns and Smarter Justice response

### LegalZoom
- Category: subscription legal services and documents
- Patterns observed: membership bundles, short attorney consultations, document templates and review, predictable pricing
- Smarter Justice adaptation: Keep optional service bundles visible only after operational gates pass; preserve fee separation and no automatic professional relationship.
- Official source reviewed: https://www.legalzoom.com/attorneys/legal-plans-overview.html

### Rocket Lawyer
- Category: membership documents and attorney access
- Patterns observed: personalized documents, e-signature and storage, questions and consultations, contract review automation
- Smarter Justice adaptation: Continue device-only preparation and exact-source tools; do not imply automated contract analysis is professional advice.
- Official source reviewed: https://www.rocketlawyer.com/

### Avvo
- Category: lawyer directory and public Q&A
- Patterns observed: large profile coverage, ratings and reviews, public legal questions, claimable profiles
- Smarter Justice adaptation: Preserve source transparency and claim/correction workflows while keeping reviews inactive until verification and moderation controls are proven.
- Official source reviewed: https://www.avvo.com/

### FindLaw and Lawyers.com
- Category: legal information and directories
- Patterns observed: topic libraries, practice-area browsing, directory comparison, English and Spanish access
- Smarter Justice adaptation: Improve plain-language path comparison and multilingual readiness without importing copyrighted articles or paid ranking logic.
- Official source reviewed: https://www.findlaw.com/

### LawHelp.org / Pro Bono Net
- Category: legal-aid and self-help referral
- Patterns observed: trusted state-by-state referrals, eligibility-oriented help, forms and self-help resources, language access
- Smarter Justice adaptation: Make nonprofit legal aid and court/agency help first-class options rather than treating private counsel as the default.
- Official source reviewed: https://www.lawhelp.org/

### LegalMatch
- Category: guided lawyer matching
- Patterns observed: structured case presentation, lawyer notifications, privacy messaging
- Smarter Justice adaptation: Use preparation tools but do not create or transmit a case, lead, or lawyer match without explicit user action and an opened routing gate.
- Official source reviewed: https://www.legalmatch.com/

### Clio Grow and Lawmatics
- Category: professional intake and CRM
- Patterns observed: custom intake forms, pipeline tracking, follow-up automation, booking, reporting, and e-signature
- Smarter Justice adaptation: Retain professional workflow foundations, but keep booking, confidential intake, and automated follow-up closed until production operations are ready.
- Official source reviewed: https://www.clio.com/features/client-intake-legal-crm/

## Approved adaptations

- Neutral Legal Help Options Navigator covering self-help, court or agency help, nonprofit legal aid, limited-scope assistance, one-time consultation, and full representation.
- Clear comparison of fit, limits, preparation steps, cost questions, and relationship boundaries.
- Owner-only competitor watchlist with dated sources and explicit non-copying rules.
- Professional profile transparency, neutral ordering, correction and claim paths, and explicit participation status remain differentiators.

## Patterns explicitly rejected

- Paid or sponsored status influencing organic directory order.
- Unverified ratings, testimonials, case results, or outcome promises.
- Automatic lead creation, lawyer notification, or confidential case transmission.
- Bundled paid services before staffing, security, payment, support, and legal gates pass.
- Copying competitor text, layouts, scoring methods, branding, or proprietary content.
