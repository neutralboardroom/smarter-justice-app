# Smarter Justice v1.7.18 No-Change Ledger

The audit deliberately did not change the following because the artifact provided no responsible basis to do so or required gates remain unavailable:

- Professional profile data, source claims, claim status, verification status, inquiry eligibility, or neutral ordering.
- Official forms, form editions, filing instructions, fees, or filing capability.
- AI providers, AI defaults, model configuration, or sensitive AI processing.
- Professional pricing, memberships, payments, subscriptions, reviews, booking, or routing.
- Human Review, attorney review, tax-professional review, or representation claims.
- Confidential uploads, persistent sensitive storage, account continuity, cross-device continuity, or automatic professional handoff.
- Production deployment or live-version claims.

These remain unchanged to preserve accuracy, safety, privacy, professional boundaries, and launch-gate integrity.
