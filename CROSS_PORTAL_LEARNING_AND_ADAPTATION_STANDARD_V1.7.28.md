# Cross-Portal Learning and Adaptation Standard — v1.7.28

## 1. Purpose

The Smarter Network must retain useful learning across Smarter Justice and every focused legal, tax, business, immigration, health, property, and money platform without erasing portal-specific requirements. A verified improvement in one portal becomes a candidate for other portals, not an automatic mandate.

## 2. Authority and implementation truth

1. The owner’s newest explicit instruction controls.
2. The newest actual authoritative portal artifact controls what is implemented.
3. Safety, privacy, security, professional responsibility, legal, ethical, licensing, and compliance requirements control over convenience or consistency.
4. Portal-specific approved decisions and boundaries control over shared patterns.
5. This registry supplies reusable evidence and candidates only.

A roadmap, conversation, prompt, screenshot, release note, or source-portal feature does not prove implementation in a target portal. The target portal must contain the feature, specialty adaptations, and appropriate tests before it may be described as implemented.

## 3. Required registry record

Every material shared capability or lesson should record:

- stable capability identifier and name;
- source portal and exact source version;
- source ZIP filename, size, SHA-256, and evidence level when available;
- user, professional, business, safety, technical, or operational problem solved;
- verified implementation and test status;
- reusable value;
- specialty adaptations;
- privacy, security, compliance, licensing, and professional boundaries;
- dependencies and operational requirements;
- owner-approval requirements;
- known defects and unresolved limitations;
- target-portal adoption status;
- completion criteria and next action.

Allowed adoption statuses are adopted, adopt now, adapt with specialty changes, verify first, defer, reject, and not applicable.

## 4. Required continuation-prompt section

Every future portal continuation prompt must include a numbered section titled **Cross-Portal Learning and Adaptation**. It must:

- identify verified outbound learning from the portal;
- identify relevant inbound learning from other portals;
- list the source portal, version, artifact identity when known, evidence status, and tests;
- classify each candidate using the approved adoption statuses;
- explain specialty adaptations, dependencies, risks, approval needs, and completion criteria;
- record rejected and not-applicable transfers with reasons;
- require the next chat to inspect the newest uploaded artifact before relying on the snapshot;
- require the next build to update the portal-specific list and shared registry;
- preserve portal-specific branding, users, architecture, deployment method, forms, professional categories, and regulated boundaries;
- exclude confidential public-user information from all shared snapshots.

## 5. Required build workflow

After every real portal ZIP audit or build:

1. Verify the exact artifact.
2. Establish actual capabilities and unresolved limits.
3. Review the latest cross-portal snapshot.
4. Identify outbound learning contributed by this portal.
5. Evaluate inbound candidates for this portal.
6. Classify each candidate.
7. Implement every safe, relevant, material, reasonably achievable adaptation.
8. Add portal-specific tests.
9. Test the exact final ZIP after fresh extraction.
10. Update the registry snapshot, adoption status, continuation prompt, and portal-specific improvement list.
11. Export non-confidential metadata to the private Smarter Ecosystem Control Center.

## 6. Portable snapshot package

Each portal should eventually package current versions of:

- `CROSS_PORTAL_CAPABILITY_SNAPSHOT.json`;
- `CROSS_PORTAL_LEARNING.md`;
- `PORTAL_CAPABILITY_ADOPTION_STATUS.json`.

These files provide portable context. They never supersede the actual portal artifact or the private central registry.

## 7. Confidentiality and platform boundaries

The registry may coordinate artifacts, releases, capabilities, tests, decisions, risks, staffing, domains, deployment truth, and next actions. It must not centralize confidential legal intake, privileged communications, medical records, financial records, account data, property-security information, credentials, secrets, or automatic copies of public-user records.

## 8. Non-copying rule

Share verified lessons and patterns. Do not blindly copy source code, public language, pricing, legal logic, professional categories, branding, forms, data-retention rules, or deployment architecture. A change is warranted only when it creates a clear evidence-based benefit and can be completed and tested responsibly in the target portal.

## 9. Current v1.7.28 limitation

Smarter Justice now packages and displays the evidence-aware registry and migration handoff. The standalone Smarter Ecosystem Control Center does not yet provide authenticated writable synchronization or append-only cross-repository history. That migration remains a high-priority next step. Until then, portal artifacts and packaged snapshots remain the operating handoff method.
