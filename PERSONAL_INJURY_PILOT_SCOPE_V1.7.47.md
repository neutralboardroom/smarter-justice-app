# Personal Injury Law Aid Pilot Scope — v1.7.47

## Owner decision

Personal Injury Law Aid is pilot three after Divorce Law Aid and Estate Law Aid. The pilot includes vehicle accidents and other personal-injury matters. Workers’ Compensation Law Aid remains separate. Car Accident Law Aid is preserved as an independent dormant compatibility asset and is not separately launched or automatically merged.

## Simple launch paths

- Cars, trucks, motorcycles, rideshare, pedestrians, bicycles, uninsured and hit-and-run injury starting paths
- Premises and slip-and-fall injuries
- Construction and worksite third-party injuries
- Defective products
- Dog bites and other negligence injuries
- Wrongful death
- Medical and nursing-home injury starting paths with specialty handoff where appropriate
- Evidence-preservation and consultation-preparation checklist
- Specialty directory and profile pages

## Safety boundaries

The pilot must not predict liability, comparative fault, serious-injury status, claim value, settlement value, deadlines, attorney acceptance, or outcomes. It must not automate demand packages, filing, representation, or insurer communications. Time-sensitive information must link to current official sources and advise prompt qualified assistance rather than state a guaranteed deadline.

## New York source basis

New York DFS describes No-Fault/PIP as personal-injury protection for drivers, passengers, and pedestrians and publishes filing guidance. New York DFS also issued July 1, 2026 guidance about material 2026 motor-vehicle insurance reforms. The New York Workers’ Compensation Board separately describes workers’ compensation for work-related injuries and illnesses. These sources support including vehicle injuries within a broad personal-injury starting portal while preserving workers’ compensation as a separate legal and operational path.
