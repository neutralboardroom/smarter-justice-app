2. The Whole Situation Map still named out-of-scope Smarter Money, Smarter Health, and Smarter Property sectors even though the Control Center had been corrected to legal-only scope.
3. Phase One directory coverage could be expanded across all six authorized priority regions with current professional-controlled sources.
4. Runtime patch currency, WCAG 2.2 acceptance, dependency audit availability, and closed external-AI/upload gates required current evidence and watch triggers.
