# Smarter Justice v1.7.25 Audit Report

## Scope
v1.7.25 converts current trust, scam-resistance, responsible-AI, and accessibility research into a narrow public verification tool and protected owner evidence ledger. It also completes the required Northern Metro New Jersey-led profile cycle.

## Implemented public innovation
- Added the device-only Trust & Verification Center.
- Added a suspicious-message workflow that encourages users to pause, preserve originals, independently locate an official contact channel, and verify urgent instructions without using only message-supplied links or numbers.
- Added an AI-assisted legal draft checklist covering jurisdiction, official sources, citations, facts, dates and service rules, privacy, local rules and AI policies, and qualified human review.
- Added local text and JSON exports.

## Public boundaries
The tool does not authenticate a message, declare a scam, review draft text, give legal advice, calculate deadlines, determine eligibility, score risk, upload or save entries, transmit entries to AI or analytics, create leads, report users or senders, notify professionals, or route information.

## Owner governance
- Added a protected Trust & Verification evidence ledger.
- Updated the Innovation Lab to record the implemented experiment, safeguards, success/failure signals, and a future evidence-provenance research candidate.
- Rejected automated authenticity verdicts, reputation lookups, risk scores, uploads before confidential-document gates pass, and automatic reporting.

## Gate status
