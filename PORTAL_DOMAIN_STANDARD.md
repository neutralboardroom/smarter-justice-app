# Smarter Justice Portal Domain Standard

Version: 1.0.0
Effective: July 20, 2026

## Purpose

Every Smarter Justice portal must keep domain ownership, DNS, SSL, deployment, canonical configuration, live verification, public availability, and professional participation as separate truthful states.

## Required domain record

Each official or proposed domain record should include:

- Stable domain-record ID.
- Stable portal identifier and any canonical portfolio identifier.
- Public brand name.
- Domain name and role, such as primary, defensive redirect, or proposed primary.
- Ownership status.
- DNS status.
- SSL status.
- Deployment status.
- Canonical status.
- Portal-development status.
- Public-user availability.
- Professional-participation status.
- Verified live URL, when applicable.
- Source or verification note.
- Private owner notes.
- Public-display permission.
- Status-history events.

## Non-negotiable distinctions

- Purchased does not mean DNS configured.
- DNS configured does not mean SSL active.
- SSL active does not mean the intended application is deployed.
- Deployment does not mean the canonical domain was verified.
- A live website does not mean paid membership, public services, professional participation, confidential traffic, or broad launch is approved.
- A proposed or recommended domain must remain private until ownership is explicitly confirmed.

## Public professional-network display

The professional-facing Smarter Justice experience may show confirmed owned domains and safe status information. It must not expose private infrastructure, credentials, internal blockers, vendor secrets, or unconfirmed purchase plans.

Each public domain card should explain:

- Official brand and domain.
- Portal or build status.
- Website availability.
- Professional-participation status.
- A truthful next action, such as visiting a verified live website, reviewing membership, or adding the portal to professional interests.

## Portal-package requirements

Each portal package and continuation prompt should record:

- Official owned domain, if confirmed.
- Whether it is primary or defensive.
- DNS, SSL, deployment, canonical, and live-verification state.
- Current production URL.
- Any redirect policy.
- Whether professional participation is open, application-only, pilot-paused, controlled, or unavailable.
- Remaining owner actions.

A portal must not claim a domain is owned, deployed, live, canonical, or open merely because a matching name appears in a roadmap or chat.
