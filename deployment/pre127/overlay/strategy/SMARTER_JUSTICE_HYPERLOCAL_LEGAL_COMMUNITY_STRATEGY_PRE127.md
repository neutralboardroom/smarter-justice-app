# Smarter Justice hyperlocal legal-community strategy

Release working paper: v2.0.0-pre127  
Date: September 1, 2026  
Status: Builder-facing strategy tied to implemented PRE127 behavior

## Executive decision

Smarter Justice is evolving from a product whose professional side could be understood as paid profile enhancement into a connected network of hyperlocal legal communities. The public still begins with a legal problem, preparation, source-linked resources, and independent professional choice. A lawyer or firm joins for recurring local intelligence, participation, professional presence, and practical tools.

The profile remains essential, but it is the free factual identity layer beneath the community. Payment must never buy accuracy, credentials, verification, endorsement, hidden organic position, or guaranteed clients.

The governing line is:

> You do not pay to exist in Smarter Justice. You join to participate.

## Attribution and founder context

The core idea—organize Smarter Justice as hyperlocal legal communities rather than primarily sell attorney profiles—is Roger's idea. Builders must preserve this attribution.

Roger also supplied the first-party origin account: the Justice Truck idea began around the Downtown Brooklyn courthouses while he was moving in and out of that area in connection with Rock and Hammer Tax Services, mobile tax-truck offices, and the office at 26 Court Street. This fact explains why Downtown Brooklyn / Civic Center is an authentic first community. It does not establish a date, court affiliation, government relationship, partnership, endorsement, or special access. Do not invent any of those claims.

## The product model

### Public promise

Help a person understand a local legal problem, locate responsible-source information, prepare, and independently decide whether to contact a professional.

### Professional promise

Help a lawyer or firm stay current locally, participate usefully, maintain a professional presence, use practice tools, and make it easier for people who independently choose them to connect.

### Identity and geography

Use one SmarterJustice.com brand, account, profile graph, firm graph, and membership relationship. Local communities are nested product spaces, not independent companies or sites.

Four geographic facts must remain separate:

| Fact | Meaning | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Office location | A sourced or professionally confirmed business-office fact | That every service area contains an office |
| Home legal community | A member's primary local professional community | An office, jurisdiction, or endorsement |
| Participating communities | Other communities the member elects to follow or join | Eligibility for every matter there |
| Service areas | Places the professional says they may serve | Current availability, engagement, conflict clearance, or jurisdiction |

Parent layers—Kings County, New York City, and New York State—can supply broader context without diluting the hyperlocal identity.

### Community launch rule

Build the smallest meaningful legal community that can stay alive. A named community needs enough attorney and firm density, local institutions, reliable content, public need, professional activity, and operational capacity to avoid an empty page. Geographic size may vary. Weak adjacent areas should remain filters or combine into a natural cluster.

PRE127 launches only Downtown Brooklyn / Civic Center as a working community. Other areas remain candidates, not public promises.

## Member lifecycle

### Day 0: understand before paying

The lawyer can find and review the free profile, see the public community, inspect the working member-home preview, compare exact plan amounts, and read the boundaries.

### Day 1: first value

The member reviews the free profile, selects a home community, selects practice areas, opens the current source-linked brief, and copies a useful LinkedIn draft if desired.

### Week 1: habit

The member returns for current local court and professional updates, follows responsible sources, uses a relevant resource, and sees participation paths. The product must provide value even if no prospective client appears.

### Month 1: retention

Retention should be earned through recurring currentness, professional relevance, participation, presence, and tools. Measure whether the member returns, uses filters, shares briefs, updates identity, and uses active workflows—not merely whether a lead appeared.

## LinkedIn distribution

Roger has a substantial professional network and can use local, source-linked briefs as a reason to post something genuinely useful to lawyers. The product supports copying a prepared post, opening LinkedIn's share flow, and copying the canonical brief link.

The exact connection/contact counts are founder-supplied estimates and are not published as verified facts. A LinkedIn connection does not authorize an email, mass direct message, scraped-list campaign, mailbox probing, or sale of contact data. PRE127 does not automate posting or outreach.

The intended loop is:

1. Human reviews the edition and responsible sources.
2. Human edits the draft in their own voice.
3. Human posts the canonical local brief to their own network.
4. Lawyers read a useful source-linked page.
5. Interested professionals inspect the community home or free profile path.
6. Product analytics, once qualified, measure aggregate page and activation behavior without exposing private matters.

## Editorial operating model

Every published item needs a stable ID, direct responsible-source URL, source name, observed date, review date, expiry when time-sensitive, audience, and correction path. Source summaries stay narrow. Court rules and notices tell lawyers to inspect the complete current source and case-specific direction.

The target is weekly, but only when qualified content exists. A missed edition is safer than manufactured content. There is no automatic publication. If a source changes, disappears, or cannot be confirmed, the item is held or expires. Corrections and retractions remain auditable.

No source listing implies partnership, endorsement, bar or court affiliation, shared responsibility, referral status, or permission to contact the source's people.

## Privacy and professional-responsibility boundaries

PRE127 uses only a member's own saved community, practice-area, and service-area selections for personalization. It does not use private public-user matters, individual demand signals, inferred sensitive interests, or matter text.

Future aggregate demand signals are not authorized merely because they could be valuable. They require a separate design with minimum cohorts, suppression, source classification, review, legal approval, and a proof that re-identification and sensitive inference risks are controlled.

Smarter Justice does not claim to be a law firm, court, government agency, bar association, certified lawyer-referral service, or an endorser of listed professionals. Contact requests and membership do not form an attorney-client relationship. A professional must independently review fit, jurisdiction, conflicts, availability, engagement terms, and professional obligations.

## Metrics and experiments

Measure the community product as a recurring-value system:

| Stage | Primary measure | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Brief visits and community-home visits | No inflated network-count claims |
| First value | Profile review, community selection, practice selection, brief opened | No private-matter analytics |
| Activation | At least two meaningful actions in seven days | Do not count passive page loads as success |
| Habit | Qualified weekly return and source opens | Content must remain current |
| Sharing | Copy/open actions and attributable visits | No automatic posting or mass messaging |
| Conversion | Free account to paid enrollment when billing is actually accepted | Never equate button click with paid activation |
| Retention | Ongoing currentness, tools, participation, and presence usage | Do not promise a client outcome |

Experiments should vary messaging, onboarding order, content packaging, and voluntary share prompts. They must not vary factual accuracy, credential display, verification, organic relevance, or professional-responsibility safeguards based on payment.

## Expansion decision

Do not launch the second named community merely to create inventory. Score candidate areas for attorney density, firm density, public catchment, courts and legal institutions, local professional activity, reliable content frequency, legal-service activity, distinct identity, and operating cost. Launch only if the editorial and product team can sustain currentness.

## Non-negotiable builder rules

- Preserve one Smarter Justice brand and connected account model.
- Preserve free factual profiles and free corrections.
- Keep office, home community, participation, service area, jurisdiction, availability, membership, and opportunity eligibility separate.
- Do not sell or imply purchased credentials, verification, endorsement, organic position, or guaranteed clients.
- Do not redesign the homepage without a separate owner decision.
- Do not publish owner network totals without verification.
- Do not automate social posting or mass outreach without a separately approved, consent-safe system.
- Do not inject private user matters into community content or professional personalization.
- Do not fabricate local content to meet a cadence.
- Do not mutate Stripe or provider configuration as part of a content/product-language release.
- Keep rollback and no-loss verification operational.
