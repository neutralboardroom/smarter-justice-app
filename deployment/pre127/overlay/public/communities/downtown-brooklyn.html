Where the idea began

Born in front of the Downtown Brooklyn courthouses.

Founder Roger’s Justice Truck idea began here while he moved in and out of the courthouse area and operated Rock and Hammer Tax Services, its mobile tax-truck offices, and the office at 26 Court Street. That direct street-level experience is why this first legal community begins with Downtown Brooklyn rather than a generic national directory page.
