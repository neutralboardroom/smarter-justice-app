## Product identity

Smarter Justice is a public legal starting-help platform with connected hyperlocal legal communities. It is one brand, one domain, one account system, and one platform. It is not a collection of separate neighborhood brands or domains.

The public comes first: people can describe a situation without choosing a legal category, use free preparation tools, find local public resources, and independently search professional profiles. The professional side is a connected layer, not a replacement for public legal help.

## The founder's strategic decision

Roger originated the decision to move Smarter Justice away from treating paid lawyer profiles as the central product and toward a network of useful hyperlocal legal-professional communities. Roger also specified that “hyperlocal” may mean parts of Brooklyn, not Brooklyn as a single final community.

The first community is Downtown Brooklyn / Civic Center. Roger supplied the first-party account that the Justice Truck idea was born around the Downtown Brooklyn courthouses while he walked to and from Rock and Hammer Tax Services, its mobile tax-truck offices, and the office at 26 Court Street. This account does not imply a courthouse, government, bar-association, or building affiliation, and no date is inferred.

## Core product laws

1. A factual professional profile is free trust infrastructure, not the paid product.
2. Payment cannot establish identity, credentials, license status, specialty, verification, office location, endorsement, or ordinary unpaid position.
3. Membership, when open, buys recurring participation and useful professional tools, not trust or guaranteed clients.
4. Office, license, home community, participating communities, service area, profile status, membership, billing, entitlement, and opportunity eligibility are separate facts.
5. One professional or firm identity may span several published communities without duplicate accounts or profiles.
6. A research candidate is not a published community, chapter, office, member group, or service area.
7. A public link to a court, bar association, nonprofit, or other organization does not imply partnership, sponsorship, endorsement, referral, approval, or shared responsibility.
8. Private user matters cannot populate public community feeds, professional personalization, demand reports, or marketing.
9. The public chooses whether to contact a professional. The professional independently decides whether to respond or accept a matter. An attorney-client relationship requires separate mutual acceptance and terms.
10. No membership promise may be marketed as active until a representative member can receive it after a verified entitlement and can cancel or lose access correctly.

## Hyperlocal model

The unit is the smallest area that can support a useful, current, responsibly operated legal-community experience. Boundaries may differ in size because legal activity is not evenly distributed. Downtown Brooklyn / Civic Center can be compact because courts, legal services, government activity, firms, and transportation are concentrated there.

The network is nested:

- Smarter Justice
- New York
- New York City
- Brooklyn / Kings County
- Downtown Brooklyn / Civic Center Legal Community

Parent places provide broader context. They are not additional memberships. Future areas remain unpublished research until current sources, clear boundaries, operating capacity, and recurring usefulness are accepted.

## Available value in PRE128

- Public legal starting help and device-only preparation tools.
- Independent professional and firm search.
- Free review of factual profiles and eligible correction paths.
- Downtown Brooklyn public legal-community page and current brief.
- Professional community preview without payment.
- Practice-area filtering based only on professional choices.
- Manual LinkedIn draft copying; no automatic posting or direct messaging.
- Saved community settings for existing verified professional accounts.

New professional registration is paused in production until transactional email can reliably verify accounts and support recovery. Existing verified accounts are preserved.

## Not active in PRE128

- Paid membership enrollment, checkout, or paid entitlements.
- “Founding” scarcity or price-protection promises.
- Member-only inquiries, referral access, or client guarantees.
- Member submissions, open comments, social-feed mechanics, peer introductions, or member-directory exports.
- Claimed chapters, boards, officers, committees, sponsors, partners, CLE approval, or referral-service status.
- Automated LinkedIn posting or outreach to Roger's network.
- Private-matter-derived professional intelligence.

## Community conduct and competition

The community cannot be used to coordinate prices or fees, allocate markets or clients, coordinate bids or client acceptance, organize boycotts, exchange confidential client or competitively sensitive information, harvest contact information, or send unwanted marketing. Member submissions and peer connections remain closed until moderation, consent, privacy, abuse prevention, appeal, and competition safeguards are operationally accepted.

## Build and release philosophy

PRE128 is a modular-monolith successor to exact production PRE127. It does not rewrite the inherited working platform. It corrects customer truth at the server and static-source layers, retires public legacy topology through explicit redirects or gone responses, preserves internal compatibility where replacement would create no user value, and keeps rollback to PRE127 exact.

The release may be source-qualified for public truth and community-preview improvements while sensitive traffic, paid commerce, or other independent lanes remain closed. Deployment and live verification are separate states and must never be inferred from a source receipt.
