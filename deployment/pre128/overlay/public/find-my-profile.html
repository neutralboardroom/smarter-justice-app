For legal professionals

Find your profile first.

Search by attorney or firm name. Review the public facts and source information, request an eligible correction or removal at no charge, and request control only after Smarter Justice can confirm your authority.

No payment or membership is required to review a profile or submit an eligible correction, suppression, duplicate, or removal request.

What happens next

  1. Open the profile that appears to match you or your firm.
  2. Check every public fact and its supporting source.
  3. Submit an eligible correction or removal request for free.
  4. If account registration is available, request authority to manage the profile.
  5. Explore the local professional community without paying.

Paid enrollment is not open. Payment never determines identity, credentials, verification, or organic position.
