Geography without fiction

Four different facts stay different.

Office location
A factual business-office claim with its own source or professional confirmation.
Home legal community
The member’s primary local professional community. Selecting it does not claim an office there.
Participating communities
Other local communities in which the member chooses to receive relevant information or participate.
Service areas
Places the professional says they may serve, subject to jurisdiction, practice fit, conflicts, availability, engagement, and professional responsibility.

Questions

Before you join

Do I have to pay to be listed or correct my profile?

No. Source-linked public facts, public business contact information, correction requests, and approved profile control remain free.

Does membership guarantee clients or inquiries?

No. People decide whom to contact, a professional decides whether to respond or accept an engagement, and no inquiry, matter, client, revenue, or result is guaranteed.

Does payment improve credentials or organic ranking?

No. Identity, credentials, profile authority, jurisdiction, specialty evidence, organic relevance, conflicts, availability, and membership are separate.

Is Smarter Justice a lawyer-referral service or bar association?

No. Smarter Justice provides legal starting information, independent professional search, local community information, and optional professional tools. It does not claim to be a court, bar association, law firm, government agency, or certified lawyer-referral service.

Can a lawyer join more than one community?

The platform is designed around one home community plus other participating communities and inherited parent-community information. Office and service-area claims remain separate.

Does sending or receiving a request create an attorney-client relationship?

No. A professional must separately review the request, clear conflicts, decide whether to accept, and provide any required engagement terms.
