# Cross-Portal Learning Packet — v1.7.53

## Recommended reusable pattern

Use one canonical public story with two presentation modes: a complete self-guided page and an optional one-step presenter view. Use fixed practice-specific short paths and static QR assets for durable field handoffs. Keep attribution separate, optional, allowlisted, and absent from the QR destination by default.

## Constraints

- A QR code does not prove a destination is deployed or current.
- Scan and print acceptance must occur on real devices before field distribution.
- Do not encode client, matter, survivor, payment, credential, staff, owner, or secret data.
- Domestic Violence demonstrations require additional survivor-safety and confidential-location controls.
- Adoption by any portal requires that portal’s own exact artifact, brand, route, accessibility, safety, and rollback acceptance.

Recommendation: **OPTIONAL** for portals with in-person professional outreach; **VERIFY FIRST** for safety-sensitive or time-sensitive destinations.
