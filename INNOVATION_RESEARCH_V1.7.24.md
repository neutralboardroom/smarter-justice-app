# Smarter Justice Innovation Research — v1.7.24

## Decision
The release prioritizes a privacy-preserving **Whole Situation Map** rather than another generic legal chatbot. Civil legal problems frequently arrive in clusters and may affect housing, income, health, safety, family, benefits, debt, business, and property at the same time. Smarter Justice can create a distinctive ecosystem advantage by helping users see those connections while leaving control of their facts with them.

## Evidence translated into design rules
- Start with the user’s whole situation, not a single legal label.
- Keep first-stage organization device-only and minimum-data.
- Explain why every sector or preparation path appears.
- Support mobile, plain-language, accessibility, and user review.
- Separate general information from legal, medical, financial, insurance, tax, mortgage, and property advice.
- Do not create a score, lead, match, or transfer from organizer answers.
- Require an owner-visible hypothesis, success measure, failure signal, safeguard, and next decision before expanding an experiment.

## Implemented experiment
The Whole Situation Map covers housing, work and income, debt and scams, health care, family and safety, benefits and disability, immigration, criminal and traffic matters, business, property, education, aging, and estates. It creates a transparent local map, questions, record suggestions, and a previewable handoff checklist. Text and JSON downloads remain local until the user chooses what to do with them.

## Deferred experiments
Source-freshness receipts are the strongest next public trust experiment. Accessible guided voice remains research-only until provider, privacy, retention, accuracy, language, accessibility, security, and human-review gates pass. Cross-sector consent receipts remain deferred until the standalone ecosystem control center and sector-specific privacy controls mature.

## Rejected shortcuts
No general-purpose AI intake inviting confidential facts, legal-risk or case-value scores, deadline predictions, automatic matching, hidden professional notification, automatic cross-sector sharing, paid organic ranking, or unverified public reviews.
