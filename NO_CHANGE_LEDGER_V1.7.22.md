# No-Change Ledger — v1.7.22

The following remain unchanged by design:
- Smarter Justice, Smarter Health, Smarter Property, and Smarter Money remain separate sector platforms with independent repositories, public identities, deployments, release histories, continuation prompts, and authoritative ZIPs.
- The new Smarter Ecosystem Control Center starter does not supersede any dedicated sector artifact.
- Exact artifact identity fields are not editable through the operating workspace.
- No confidential public-user records are centralized or copied between sectors by default.
- Production remains last verified at v1.6.1.
- Sensitive traffic is not approved.
- Professional paid pilot and public paid Human Review remain paused.
- Payments, subscriptions, booking, public reviews, confidential uploads, unrestricted professional routing, broad cross-sector sharing, and automatic filing remain closed.
- New profiles remain unclaimed, credential-unverified, nonparticipating, unpaid, unavailable, and inquiry-ineligible.
- Runtime dependencies remain `nodemailer` and `pg`; development dependencies remain zero.
