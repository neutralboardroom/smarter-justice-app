15A. GENERATED-EVIDENCE REPRODUCIBILITY, NON-IDEMPOTENT OUTCOMES, FLAKY TESTS, AND LEGAL-USE TRUTH
===============================================================================

DO NOT CONFUSE TWO DIFFERENT FORMS OF IDEMPOTENCY

State-changing idempotency means safely repeating an operation does not create
duplicate or inconsistent state. Evidence-generation repeatability means an
accepted input and execution boundary produces the same or an explicitly
bounded equivalent result. A workflow may satisfy one and fail the other. Test
and record both where applicable.

REQUIRED EVIDENCE REPEATABILITY CLASS

Classify each material evidence generator as exactly one of:

- BYTE-DETERMINISTIC — independent clean executions produce byte-identical
  output;
- CANONICALLY EQUIVALENT — volatile nonmeaningful fields are removed under a
  documented canonicalization rule and remaining evidence is identical;
- SEMANTICALLY BOUNDED STOCHASTIC — wording or ordering may vary, but required
  facts, citations, constraints, schemas, and invariants stay inside a defined
  tested boundary;
- EXTERNAL-STATE DEPENDENT — results depend on a named current source,
  provider, registry, network, clock, law, or environment and are bound to
  retrieval and observation evidence;
- HUMAN-JUDGMENT DEPENDENT — a qualified human decision is part of the evidence
  and the reviewer, criteria, inputs, conflicts, limits, and receipt are
  recorded; or
- NOT ACCEPTABLE AS SOLE EVIDENCE — the output may assist investigation or
  drafting but cannot independently prove the asserted fact, requirement,
  legal conclusion, implementation, acceptance, or live state.

Do not use `deterministic`, `reproducible`, `idempotent`, `verified`, or
`repeatable` without identifying the class, exact boundary, comparison method,
and evidence scope.

GENERATION-BINDING RECORD

For each material evidence-producing run, record as applicable:

- generator, command, script, test, workflow, model, template, prompt, schema,
  policy, and version;
- exact source artifact, tree, patch, inputs, data snapshot, and hashes;
- provider, model revision, endpoint, runtime, package, dependency, lockfile,
  OS, architecture, hardware, and container identity;
- temperature, top-p, seed, decoding mode, locale, timezone, clock source,
  concurrency, ordering, network, cache, retry, and external-state controls;
- source URLs or identifiers, retrieval time, response digest, effective date,
  jurisdiction, and currentness scope;
- nonsecret configuration and feature flags;
- canonicalization and permitted-variance rules;
- run ID, actor, environment, timestamps, output files and hashes; and
- acceptance invariants, observed variance, result, invalidation triggers,
  rollback, and revalidation date.

A fixed seed, temperature zero, cache hit, identical prompt, or provider claim
does not by itself prove reproducibility across provider updates, hardware,
libraries, platforms, or time.

NON-IDEMPOTENT OUTCOME TEST

For every claimed deterministic or repeatable evidence path:

1. reset mutable state, caches, temporary files, databases, queues, clocks, and
   environment variables to the documented clean boundary;
2. run the identical accepted command and inputs independently at least twice,
   and more often when risk or known variability requires it;
3. compare bytes or the documented canonical form;
4. compare all evidence-relevant facts, statuses, counts, citations, decisions,
   and invariants;
5. record `PASS IDENTICAL`, `PASS CANONICAL EQUIVALENCE`, `PASS BOUNDED
   VARIANCE`, or `NON-IDEMPOTENT OUTCOME DETECTED`;
6. preserve every run and invalidate the affected evidence and gate when a
   material difference occurs; and
7. diagnose, remove, or explicitly bound the variance before acceptance.

Never keep only a favorable run, rerun until success, hide a conflicting
result, silently alter seeds, reuse an unrecorded cache, suppress failure,
weaken assertions, widen tolerances without evidence, or use majority voting to
manufacture acceptance.

FLAKY-TEST AND VARIABLE-PIPELINE QUARANTINE

Detect order, state pollution, randomness, clock, timezone, locale, network,
concurrency, port, filesystem, resource, retry, and external-service dependence.
Use isolated state, deterministic fixtures, controlled clocks, explicit seeds
where supported, collision-safe resources, and authenticated mocks or recorded
responses where appropriate. Mark unresolved variable tests or pipelines
`QUARANTINED — NOT ACCEPTANCE EVIDENCE`; keep dependent gates closed or at the
last exact accepted state.

AI AND GENERATED OUTPUT

AI output may support research leads, brainstorming, summaries, organization,
drafts, comparison, missing-information detection, and human review. It does
not prove an underlying fact, law, source currentness, professional status,
product behavior, test result, artifact identity, acceptance, or admissibility.
Preserve exact prompts and available provider/model/sampling state, verify

is admissible, authentic, expert, or sufficient for a court, agency, insurer,

21. LEARNING LINEAGE, TARGET ACCEPTANCE, AND PROPAGATION CONTROL
===============================================================================

Cross-platform learning must follow this lifecycle:

1. SOURCE ARTIFACT IDENTIFIED.
2. SOURCE ARTIFACT EVIDENCE CLASSIFIED.
3. LEARNING CANDIDATE EXTRACTED.

6. PROMPT DISPOSITION RECORDED.
7. TARGET IMPLEMENTATION COMPLETED.
8. TARGET-SPECIFIC TESTS AND ACCEPTANCE COMPLETED.
9. TARGET RELEASE EXACTLY VERIFIED.
10. TARGET LEARNING ELIGIBLE FOR FURTHER PROPAGATION.

A prompt instruction is not implementation. Code presence is not acceptance. A
passing unit test is not automatically product acceptance. A target adaptation
may not be presented as verified in that target until the target's exact
artifact and target-specific acceptance evidence support it.

- REVALIDATED AND IMPROVED;
- CHANGED — NEW RULE OR IMPLEMENTATION;
- SUPERSEDED;
- REMOVED INTENTIONALLY;
- REMOVED WITH UNCLEAR REASON;
- REGRESSED OR BROKEN;
- NOT RECHECKED; or
- NOT APPLICABLE TO THE NEW VERSION.

Only current, revalidated learning may drive a new prompt or build as verified
learning.

Use:

- UNIVERSAL SHARED PRACTICE;
- SMARTER JUSTICE CENTRAL-ONLY PRACTICE;
- PORTAL-SHARED PRACTICE;
- SPECIALTY-SPECIFIC PRACTICE;
- SAFETY-RESTRICTED PRACTICE;
- PRODUCT-IDENTITY-SPECIFIC PRACTICE;
- EXPERIMENTAL OR NOT YET VERIFIED;
- BLOCKED OR INCOMPLETE;
- DEPRECATED;
- REJECTED ANTI-PATTERN; or

TARGET DISPOSITIONS

Use:

- ADOPT AS VERIFIED;
- ADAPT FOR SMARTER JUSTICE;

- DEFER WITH GENUINE DEPENDENCY;
