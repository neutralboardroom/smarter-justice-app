# Roger Rule — Proactive Page-by-Page Audit and Improvement

Owner authority: Roger. Permanent unless Roger explicitly changes or revokes it.

Every current and future Smarter Justice build, builder, successor version, continuation packet, deployment-readiness pass, and material release must independently audit each page that is built, inherited, reviewed, materially touched, shown by Roger, or included in the release. Roger is not responsible for identifying every defect or improvement opportunity. Screenshots and owner comments are additional evidence and requirements, not the limit of builder responsibility.

Each audit must, as applicable, review design quality; layout; spacing; typography; hierarchy; navigation; links; buttons; forms; tables; cards; responsive behavior; keyboard/focus behavior; accessibility; loading/empty/error/success/authentication/recovery states; public clarity; attorney and law-firm usability; owner/admin usability; privacy and security; national U.S. positioning; jurisdiction-specific accuracy; English/Spanish parity; source/currentness presentation; performance; and consistency with the Smarter Justice design system.

Builders must proactively fix worthwhile defects and improvements they discover without waiting for Roger to enumerate them. Preserve elements that already work well. Do not make changes merely for novelty. When a page-specific defect reveals a shared component or design-system problem, fix the reusable cause where safely applicable and regression-test previously working pages.

Whenever Roger provides screenshots, audit the entire visible page, record the owner-stated concern, independently identify additional qualified issues, determine whether shared/global causes exist, add the findings to the current Next Version Improvement List, implement qualified corrections, and re-review after correction.

Automated tests alone are not sufficient to declare a material release complete. The builder must perform the maximum reasonable page-by-page visual, functional, responsive, accessibility, and workflow audit possible and document reviewed routes, findings, fixes, shared improvements, regressions checked, and any material deferrals with reasons.

This rule incorporates and is constrained by all other Roger Rules, including national 50-state + D.C. scope, no-loss/no-drift, evidence/provenance, safety/privacy, and Maximum Reasonable Material Work. It never authorizes fabrication, unsafe shortcuts, lower evidence standards, unlawful collection, privacy violations, or regressions.

No builder-generated prompt, consolidation layer, refactor, summary, automation, optimization, or successor architecture may silently omit, weaken, reinterpret, replace, or supersede this Roger Rule. Only Roger may explicitly amend or revoke it.
