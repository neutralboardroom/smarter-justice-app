# Smarter Justice Document Help Center Standard — v1.7.52

Smarter Justice coordinates a shared document-help taxonomy, safety boundary, official-source currentness model, and portal-readiness matrix. It does not duplicate specialty tools inside the umbrella repository or claim that an independent portal adopted a capability without exact artifact evidence.

## Separate user entry points

Use distinct entry points when that improves clarity, safety, completion, testing, or support:

1. Draft a letter or notice.
2. Review a document.
3. Compare documents.
4. Prepare or review an agreement.
5. Find a current official form.
6. Prepare information for a current official form.
7. Fill a controlled copy of a specific current form.
8. Organize documents and facts for an attorney.
9. Prepare an attorney-review package.

## Shared truth rules

- Browser acceptance of a filename extension is not proof of format support.
- Every supported format needs explicit size, validation, parsing, preview, failure, privacy, retention, deletion, accessibility, malicious-file, and processing-location rules.
- Confidential uploads and external AI remain closed unless separately approved, secured, integrated, tested, and supported.
- Electronic filing is not offered or implied.
- Official forms require the issuing authority, identifier, revision or edition, official source URL, retrieval date, verification date, jurisdiction, and replacement state.
- A prepared document is not attorney review. A completed form is not electronic filing.
- Private legal documents are prohibited from analytics, ordinary logs, URLs, public demonstration records, profile exports, portfolio records, fixtures, and version-control history.

## Repository boundary

The accompanying `DOCUMENT_HELP_CAPABILITY_MATRIX_V1.7.52.json` is coordination evidence only. Portal builders must verify their own exact artifacts, preserve specialty-specific safeguards, and implement only complete material improvements in their dedicated repositories.
