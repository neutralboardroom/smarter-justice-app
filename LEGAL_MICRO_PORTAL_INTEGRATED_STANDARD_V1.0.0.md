# Legal Micro-Portal Integrated Profile-Growth, Design, Brand, and Release-Quality Standard

- Version: 1.0.0
- Effective date: 2026-07-27
- Status: Owner-approved and mandatory
- Source: Controlling Smarter Justice continuation prompt after v1.7.36, Audit Revision 2
- Source-file verification boundary: The separately named owner-uploaded source file was not supplied in this build; this canonical copy preserves the complete controlling text embedded in the approved continuation prompt.

This standard was supplied and approved by Roger after v1.7.36. It is mandatory
for Smarter Justice internal requirements and every future legal micro-portal
continuation prompt, build prompt, audit, release gate, profile-growth plan,
design review, and coordination handoff.

It supplements, and does not replace:

- Roger’s newest instruction;
- exact target-portal artifact truth;
- legal, ethical, privacy, security, accessibility, and professional-
  responsibility requirements;
- specialty-specific portal requirements;
- cooperative legal-portal learning;
- evidence-gated exact-artifact testing;
- storage conservation;
- owner approval gates.

DUAL MISSION

Every appropriate legal portal build must materially improve both:

1. the number and quality of real, accurate, useful, individually supported,
   publicly claimable professional profiles; and
2. the complete product’s polish, clarity, usability, accessibility,
   responsiveness, reliability, security, performance, and release readiness.

Do not stop at plans, mockups, empty importers, demonstrations, or token profile
batches when substantially more responsible work can be completed.

ASSIGNED-PORTAL SCOPE

Work only on the assigned legal portal and its newest authoritative artifact.
Do not merge or rebuild another portal unless Roger explicitly instructs it.

Keep the portal focused on its own subject, audience, geography, terminology,
eligibility, intake, directory, and evidence rules.

Shared systems may be adapted only when they fit. Do not copy unrelated
content, claims, data, branding, workflows, or legal assumptions.

FAMILY DESIGN

All legal portals should be recognizable members of the Smarter Justice family:

- bright-white or near-white foundation;
- coordinated primary brand family;
- restrained portal accent;
- related professional typography;
- consistent spacing, navigation, buttons, forms, icons, notices, tables, and
  interaction states;
- coordinated logo and favicon family;
- similar tone, trust signals, profile structure, claim flow, accessibility,
  and responsive behavior;
- equivalent polish across public, professional, firm, and admin surfaces.

Each portal keeps a subtle subject identity. It must not look like a renamed
clone.

VISUAL DISCIPLINE

Use clean, bright, modern, calm, trustworthy, uncluttered, professional,
highly readable design.

Avoid dark heavy backgrounds, generic templates, loud gradients, excessive
color blocks, oversized heroes, huge headings, excessive shadows,
glassmorphism, decorative animation, excessive rounded containers, repetitive
card grids, childish illustrations, busy dashboards, dense walls of text,
inconsistent spacing, tiny text, oversized controls, and decoration competing
with legal information.

Use whitespace without wasting space or causing excessive scrolling.

NO DECORATIVE PILLS OR OVALS

Do not use nonfunctional pill boxes, capsules, rounded chips, bubbles, or oval
labels. They are acceptable only as real links, buttons, filters, selectable
options, meaningful statuses, or interactive controls.

Use clean checkmarks and straightforward text for features, benefits,
qualifications, services, eligibility, confirmations, and trust points.

BRAND ASSETS

Create, refine, and install a professional subject-appropriate logo and favicon
that fit the Smarter Justice family, remain legible at small sizes, work in
desktop/mobile/print/email/social contexts, and include required variants.

Do not leave placeholders, framework defaults, unrelated branding, broken image
references, or missing assets.

TYPOGRAPHY, COLOR, LAYOUT, AND COMPONENTS

Define readable typography for titles, headings, body text, navigation,
buttons, forms, helper text, notices, tables, profile metadata, dashboards, and
legal disclaimers.

Use accessible contrast and do not rely on color alone.

Use balanced spacing and alignment. Prefer headings, dividers, lists, and
structured text over unnecessary containers. Use cards for genuine discrete
objects, not every section.

Primary, secondary, and destructive actions must be visually and behaviorally
distinct.

NAVIGATION AND CLARITY

Users must understand:

- where they are;
- what the portal does;
- what information is needed;
- what action comes next;
- what happens after submission;
- how to continue or return;
- how to get help;
- how to claim, correct, or remove a profile;
- whether information is public, private, verified, unclaimed, sponsored,
  participating, or pending.

Simplify confusing flows while preserving working behavior.

COMPLETE SURFACE AUDIT

Audit every applicable:

- homepage;
- information page;
- practice page;
- location page;
- directory;
- search, filter, sort, and pagination;
- profile;
- claim, correction, suppression, and removal flow;
- intake and eligibility flow;
- referral or contact flow;
- public account;
- professional account;
- firm account;
- owner/admin tool;
- evidence-review tool;
- candidate and enrichment queue;
- import and quality tool;
- navigation and footer;
- form and validation;
- table, menu, modal, notification, and alert;
- empty, loading, error, success, and confirmation state;
- login, signup, password, email-ready, and printable view;
- mobile and tablet layout;
- SEO metadata, structured data, sitemap, and canonical behavior.

Do not polish only the homepage.

PROFILE-GROWTH OBJECTIVE

Substantially increase real, accurate, individual, publicly claimable profiles
whenever evidence permits.

Do not stop after a demonstration batch when official structured data and
first-party evidence support more.

Never generate volume from:

- invention;
- duplicates;
- unsupported practice assumptions;
- firm-only padding;
- placeholders;
- thin pages;
- lowered verification standards;
- search-result snippets used as final evidence;
- AI guesses;
- stale conflicts;
- counting candidates, firms, organizations, rejections, or suppressed records
  as published attorneys.

Every published individual attorney requires attorney-specific evidence
connecting the person to the portal’s subject.

NEW YORK STRUCTURED FOUNDATION

Use official New York State Attorney Registrations dataset `eqw2-r5nb` as the
primary high-volume New York source for identity, registration, location, and
public business information.

Use official structured export, API, OData, CSV, JSON, or another supported
method rather than manually scraping thousands of pages.

The dataset may support:

- registration number;
- name;
- registration status;
- firm or company;
- public business address;
- city, state, ZIP, and county;
- public business telephone;
- law school;
- admission year;
- judicial department or admission information.

Registration does not prove practice area, current client availability, portal
participation, or willingness to accept referrals.

Use official attorney search or another official source for clarification,
conflict resolution, or higher-confidence individual validation when needed.

SOURCE HIERARCHY

Tier 1:

- official government;
- court;
- attorney-registration and licensing systems;
- state open data;
- public discipline;
- official agency or provider directories;
- USPTO records for exact patent-practitioner roles;
- VA accreditation for exact veterans roles;
- EOIR recognized organization/accredited representative records for exact
  immigration roles;
- U.S. Trustee or other official specialty sources only for the role they
  establish.

Tier 2:

- official firm websites;
- individual professional biographies;
- practice-group pages;
- attorney-owned professional sites;
- legal-aid/nonprofit sites;
- government provider pages;
- local bar directories;
- permitted professional-organization pages.

Tier 3:

- commercial directories;
- search engines;
- news;
- court opinions;
- reputable discovery sources.

Use Tier 3 to discover stronger sources. Do not use it as sole authority for
licensing, discipline, specialty, availability, or current contact when
stronger sources exist.

Respect terms, robots rules, rate limits, copyright, authentication, CAPTCHAs,
paywalls, technical protections, and permitted uses. Do not bypass controls.

ATTORNEY-SPECIFIC PRACTICE EVIDENCE

Acceptable evidence may include:

- official attorney biography expressly naming the relevant practice;
- individual listing in the firm’s relevant practice group;
- official specialty registration or accreditation;
- professional organization page identifying the work;
- government or nonprofit provider page identifying the relevant role;
- multiple recent reliable sources consistently identifying the practice;
- another direct and traceable individual-level source.

Do not assign a practice because:

- the firm offers it;
- another firm attorney offers it;
- the firm name has a keyword;
- the attorney appeared once in an old matter;
- a search snippet suggests it;
- a generic directory category includes it;
- an AI model predicts it;
- the attorney is licensed or nearby.

Firm-level evidence supports an individual only when a reliable source also
associates that individual with the relevant team, practice, service, or role.

Retain insufficient candidates in enrichment/review rather than publish them.

GEOGRAPHIC AND BATCH PROCESSING

Follow the portal’s approved geography.

Current profile-growth program historically prioritizes:

Phase One:
- Brooklyn;
- Manhattan;
- Queens;
- the Bronx;
- Northern Metro New Jersey connected to NYC;
- NYC-connected Connecticut.

Work across the authorized Phase One areas according to yield and documented
gaps. Do not remain in a low-yield area merely to satisfy a rigid quota.

Phase Two:
Substantially complete New York State for the specific portal.

Phase Three:
Only after statewide New York completion, expand state by state under the
current approved order.

Existing valid out-of-phase profiles may remain, but do not use easier outside
markets to evade current geographic work.

For every batch:

1. import official records structurally;
2. review or exclude inactive, ambiguous, disqualifying, or concerning status;
3. normalize names, firms, addresses, phones, county, ZIP, and IDs;
4. deduplicate by registration number, jurisdiction, normalized identity, and
   other reliable keys;
5. match official attorney and firm sources;
6. locate attorney-specific practice evidence;
7. enrich with permitted public professional information;
8. store field-level evidence, provenance, checked date, and confidence;
9. publish qualifying records as clearly unclaimed;
10. retain incomplete records for enrichment;
11. record rejection, conflict, suppression, and duplicate reasons;
12. continue until source, reasonable build capacity, or quality controls are
    genuinely exhausted.

Do not fabricate missing fields.

PROFILE STATE MODEL

Keep separate:

- candidate;
- enrichment pending;
- evidence review required;
- verified for publication;
- published unclaimed;
- claim pending;
- claimed and verified;
- correction pending;
- suppressed or removed;
- rejected;
- sponsored;
- participating;
- payment;
- referral availability;
- last verified;
- refresh due.

Claimable does not mean joined, endorsed, paid, participating, available, or
accepting referrals.

PROFILE DATA MODEL

Support, when verified and available:

- full name;
- professional role;
- firm or organization;
- public business address;
- city, state, ZIP, and county;
- public business phone;
- official website;
- public business email;
- relevant practice areas;
- languages;
- jurisdictions or admissions;
- registration/license identifier;
- registration status;
- admission year;
- law school;
- specialty accreditation;
- attorney-specific practice evidence;
- source records, URLs, and identifiers;
- source checked dates;
- field-level provenance;
- confidence;
- claim state;
- last verified date;
- refresh/staleness;
- correction, claim, and removal controls.

Keep source-derived and claimant-supplied information distinct.

Do not publish dates of birth, Social Security numbers, residential addresses,
protected information, or unnecessary personal data. Prefer public business
contact. Public availability does not justify every republication.

PUBLIC PROFILE PAGE

Where supported, include name, role, firm, location/service area, verified
practice, public business contact, jurisdictions, education, languages,
credentials, claim state, last verified, source-derived disclosure,
claim/correction/removal controls, and relevant portal resources.

Do not display unsupported ratings, rankings, awards, badges, endorsements,
“best lawyer” labels, guaranteed results, or fabricated testimonials.

Use scannable headings, concise sections, checkmarks, and clean metadata.

SEARCH, FILTERS, SEO, AND STRUCTURED DATA

Where source data supports them, provide practice, state, county, city, ZIP,
language, claim/verification, credential, name, firm, service mode, and
jurisdiction filters; accurate result counts; stable pagination; mobile
filters; empty-result guidance; and useful practice/location pages.

Do not imply that search order, featured status, or sponsorship is a
professional recommendation.

Use unique accurate titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemaps, accurate
structured data, stable profile URLs, redirects, and useful non-spam location
and practice pages.

Prevent indexing thin, duplicate, rejected, or incomplete pages. Avoid keyword
stuffing and repetitive generated copy. Distinguish individuals, firms, and
organizations. Structured data must match visible verified content.

TRUTHFULNESS

Never invent or assume:

- practice;
- language;
- email;
- website;
- availability;
- free consultation;
- price;
- hours;
- awards;
- results;
- certification;
- endorsement;
- membership;
- accessibility;
- sponsorship;
- participation;
- referral availability;
- new-client status;
- partnership;
- guarantee;
- ranking.

Do not convert “currently registered” into specialist, portal approved,
recommended, or guaranteed good standing.

Do not claim an unclaimed professional joined, partnered, paid, endorsed, or
accepted referrals.

CLAIM, CORRECTION, AND REMOVAL

Provide clear claim, correction, enhancement, dispute, suppression, and removal
paths.

Verify claims using official-domain email, contact already published on an
official professional site, registration/accreditation plus admin review, or
another secure method proving authority.

Knowing public information is not enough.

Keep an audit trail and distinguish claimant-supplied facts from source facts.

Provide administrative review for disputes, conflicts, impersonation,
duplicates, sensitive corrections, suppression, and removal.

Correction and removal do not require payment.

SCALABLE TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Support structured CSV, spreadsheet, API, OData, JSON, pagination, large
datasets, geographic/practice batching, checkpoints, resumability,
deterministic normalization, source-aware updates, deduplication, entity
matching, evidence states, field confidence, source priority, freshness,
validation, logs, suppression, caching, retries, idempotency, conflict
preservation, metrics, audit, and repeatable tests.

Do not overwrite stronger data with weaker or older data.

When internet/source access exists, actually process and verify profiles rather
than only build an importer. When blocked, complete safe preparation, record
the exact blocker, and never fabricate.

EVIDENCE, CONFIDENCE, FRESHNESS, AND CONFLICTS

Distinguish:

- official identity/license evidence;
- official status evidence;
- first-party affiliation evidence;
- attorney-specific practice evidence;
- contact evidence;
- language evidence;
- specialty accreditation;
- claimant-supplied evidence;
- secondary discovery.

Use field-level confidence where practical. Consider authority, directness,
freshness, consistency, and conflicts.

A high candidate score does not replace minimum publication evidence.

Store source checked dates and refresh rules. Revalidate when registration
status, firm, address, phone, website, practice evidence, claim, correction,
discipline, suspension, resignation, death, or authoritative conflict changes.

Do not silently overwrite conflicts. Preserve them and route material
uncertainty to review.

ADMINISTRATIVE TOOLS

Support candidate review, field-level evidence, source comparison, publication
approval/rejection, claim review, correction/removal, duplicate merge,
suppression, import history, retry, stale monitoring, coverage, audit export,
and separate sponsorship/participation review.

Admin interfaces must be efficient and understandable, not developer dumps.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN, ACCESSIBILITY, PERFORMANCE, SECURITY, AND RELIABILITY

Test navigation, wrapping, profiles, search, filters, forms, buttons, touch
targets, tables, dashboards, modals, spacing, overflow, mobile keyboards,
images, logos, sticky items, and long values across desktop, tablet, and phone.

Provide contrast, labels, keyboard access, focus, headings, alt text,
accessible validation, required indicators, screen-reader controls, reduced
motion, descriptive links, and non-color-only status.

Improve page load, queries, pagination, search, assets, error handling,
validation, authentication, authorization, claim verification, permissions,
audit, rate limiting, safe imports, secrets, duplicate-submission protection,
claim-abuse protection, and recovery from partial imports.

Do not expose credentials, administrative tokens, unpublished candidates,
protected information, or private review notes.

IMPROVEMENT DISCIPLINE

Preserve all working features and verified content. Do not redesign, remove,
rename, reorganize, or rebuild merely for novelty.

Avoid unnecessary dependencies, complexity, regressions, inconsistency, and
maintenance burden.

Expand only when useful, complete, appropriate, evidence supported, and
supportable.

REQUIRED REPORTING

Every portal build reports:

- new individual public profiles;
- materially updated profiles;
- total public claimable profiles;
- new and total profiles by state;
- new and total profiles by county or equivalent;
- new and total profiles by practice area;
- claimed and unclaimed counts;
- candidates awaiting evidence;
- candidates awaiting manual review;
- rejected records and reasons;
- duplicates prevented, merged, or suppressed;
- conflicts;
- percentage with official licensing/registration evidence;
- percentage with attorney-specific practice evidence;
- percentage with first-party evidence;
- percentage with public business contact;
- source systems and retrieval dates;
- import and enrichment batches;
- source-access, quality, legal, and technical limitations;
- visual, accessibility, performance, and release-quality improvements;
- automated and manual tests;
- remaining limitations.

Do not count firms, organizations, duplicate offices, candidates, rejects,
suppressed records, internal enrichment records, placeholders, or thin
unpublished pages as new public attorneys.

A zero or token profile build must state exact sources attempted, candidate
volume, rejection reasons, and evidence blocker. “More research is needed” is
not sufficient when reliable structured data and first-party evidence are
available.

FINAL TESTING AND DELIVERY

Test family consistency, portal identity, bright-white visual quality,
typography, spacing, absence of nonfunctional pills, logo/favicon,
responsiveness, navigation, search, filters, profiles, claims, corrections,
removals, forms, authentication, accounts, admin review, imports, enrichment,
deduplication, evidence, state handling, links, assets, empty/loading/error/
success states, accessibility, performance, security, runtime errors,
regressions, data quality, and release readiness.

Test from clean extraction and test the final packaged artifact.

Deliver the complete artifact, correct version, release notes, concise
implementation summary, exact profile/evidence metrics, sources and dates,
tests, limitations, manifests, and independent exact-artifact checks.

Do not leave reasonably achievable work as mockups, disconnected screens,
placeholders, empty importers, unrun tools, recommendations only, token samples,
or unfinished tasks.

The controlling outcome is both dense, accurate, individually supported
professional coverage and a cohesive, bright-white, modern, trustworthy,
responsive, accessible, release-ready portal.
