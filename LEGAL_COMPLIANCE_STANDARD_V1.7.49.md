# Smarter Justice Professional Growth Legal-Compliance Standard — v1.7.49

## Status

Development control standard. Paid sponsored visibility, case-opportunity access, live payments, and live portal distribution remain closed until the applicable legal, operational, technical, and owner gates are accepted.

## Controlling business model

- Attorneys and firms may claim, verify, correct, and edit a basic profile without payment.
- Optional paid products may add clearly labeled sponsored visibility, enhanced prominence, lead eligibility, and access to case or attorney-review opportunities.
- Payment never establishes identity, credentials, practice-area evidence, portal eligibility, staff approval, endorsement, availability, organic rank, or professional quality.
- Smarter Justice may use disclosed fixed subscription, advertising, platform, or fixed opportunity-access charges.
- Smarter Justice must not charge a percentage of legal fees, make charges contingent on retention, recovery, settlement, outcome, or attorney fees, or sell verification or organic trust signals.

## Fail-closed requirements before monetization

1. Jurisdiction-specific attorney-advertising review is recorded.
2. Jurisdiction-specific review of fee sharing, referral arrangements, opportunity charges, and professional independence is recorded.
3. Every paid placement is clearly and prominently labeled “Sponsored” or with another counsel-approved term that users can understand immediately.
4. Paid placement is visually distinguishable without implying that the attorney is “best,” certified, endorsed, or more qualified.
5. Organic profile ordering, risk guidance, credential warnings, and specialty eligibility remain payment-neutral.
6. The responsible lawyer or law firm and required contact information appear wherever applicable.
7. No unlawful real-time solicitation, prohibited targeted outreach, misleading communication, guarantee, unverifiable comparison, or unsupported specialization claim is used.
8. Case-opportunity access uses a counsel-approved fixed-fee structure and does not depend on whether the attorney accepts the matter or earns a fee.
9. Public-user information is minimized and is not shared with a professional without appropriate notice, consent, security, and purpose limitation.
10. Stripe, refunds, cancellation, support, complaints, monitoring, backup, restore, incident handling, and exact rollback are independently accepted.
11. Roger records explicit go/no-go approval for each paid gate.

## Evidence basis reviewed for this development standard

- New York Rules of Professional Conduct Rule 7.1 and Rule 7.3, as amended effective June 1, 2026, through the official New York courts order and rule text.
- New York Rule 5.4 professional-independence and fee-sharing principles, including official New York court discussion of prohibited sharing of legal fees with nonlawyers.
- Federal Trade Commission native-advertising guidance requiring advertising disclosures to be clear, prominent, and understandable near the advertising claim.
- Current official Justia and Avvo materials showing free profile claiming/editing separated from paid promotional products; these competitor materials inform product design but do not establish legal compliance.

## Required counsel review

This development artifact does not provide a legal opinion and must not be treated as jurisdiction-complete. Qualified counsel must review the actual pricing, contracts, opportunity mechanics, advertising presentation, privacy flow, jurisdictions served, and launch configuration before any paid feature opens.
