# Smarter Justice v1.7.24 Audit Report

## Scope
Audited the authoritative v1.7.23 base and implemented a focused innovation release without reopening production, payment, sensitive-data, booking, review, upload, routing, sharing, or filing gates.

## Findings and improvements
1. **Connected-problem gap:** Existing tools helped with a selected path but did not give users a safe way to identify connected housing, money, health, safety, family, benefits, business, property, education, criminal, immigration, and estate concerns. Added the device-only Whole Situation Map.
2. **Innovation governance gap:** Product ideas were distributed across research and queues without a single hypothesis/safeguard/decision structure. Added the protected owner Innovation Lab.
3. **Cross-sector privacy risk:** A connected ecosystem could encourage premature centralization. The new tool uses transparent local rules, no network request, no storage, no analytics, no AI transmission, no lead creation, and no automatic cross-sector sharing.
4. **Marketplace rotation:** Completed the required Manhattan-led official-source profile cycle.
