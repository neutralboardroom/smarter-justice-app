# Smarter Justice Portal Continuation Prompt Standard

Version 1.4.0

Every focused portal handoff prompt must preserve both the current Smarter Justice shared-system rules and the complete portal-specific source of truth. Generic ecosystem language must never erase specialty requirements, and specialty history must not weaken shared privacy, security, truthfulness, source verification, consent, or professional boundaries.

## Automatic Master Rules Pack inheritance

Every continuation prompt, replacement prompt for a long or unstable chat, manifest, and build handoff automatically embeds or references the current Master Rules and Suggestions Pack with version and SHA-256 checksum. The owner should not need to request this again.


## Professional directory and acquisition evidence

When a portal uses the central professional marketplace, its handoff must record the default directory page size, pagination or show-more behavior, individual and firm profile coverage, public-source status, claim and account-control tests, campaign attribution, membership checkout status, and narrow-phone acquisition review. A successful single-record test is not sufficient evidence that a multi-record directory or firm finder works.

## Official-source and onboarding evidence

Where a portal uses public records, professional profiles, or a marketplace, the handoff must record source availability behavior, default result size, offset or cursor pagination, filter preservation, clear controls, public URL safety, profile metadata, claim continuity, onboarding checklist status, mobile autofill review, firm pricing transparency, and free-versus-paid pricing clarity.


## Mandatory integrated legal-portal standard — v1.0.0

Every complete portal continuation prompt must include or exactly incorporate `LEGAL_MICRO_PORTAL_INTEGRATED_STANDARD_V1.0.0.md`. It must preserve the dual mission of individually supported professional-profile growth and complete product quality; assigned-portal exact-artifact authority; family design and portal identity; the no-decorative-pill rule; complete-surface review; attorney-specific practice evidence; the New York `eqw2-r5nb` structured identity foundation where applicable; maximum-yield geographic rotation; exact profile/evidence metrics; responsive, accessibility, performance, security, and reliability acceptance; final exact-artifact testing; and honest reporting of zero results and blockers.

Every prompt must also carry the complete current Next Version Improvement List. Items may be removed only when completed and verified, explicitly rejected by the owner, or superseded with recorded evidence.

## Prompt completeness rule

A Control Center-generated prompt is a governed starting point. Before use, the active portal chat must merge the newest complete specialty decisions, forms, workflows, version history, code findings, unresolved issues, and deployment facts. The latest complete prompt explicitly approved by the owner remains authoritative unless deliberately superseded.

## Professional acquisition inheritance

Every relevant portal prompt preserves public-source seeding, truthful unclaimed status, central Smarter Justice profile management, owner-approved claims, paid membership eligibility, NYC founding-member pilot, Stripe-confirmed subscriptions, mobile/QR enrollment, firm per-seat billing and discounts, campaign attribution, and strict separation of payment from AI analysis, verification, ratings, and neutral eligibility. State any justified specialty adaptation.


## Shared origin-story inheritance

Every portal handoff must preserve this owner-approved sentence:

> Smarter Justice grew from years of street-level outreach through Justice Truck—meeting people where they were, listening to their legal concerns, speaking with attorneys, and seeing how difficult it could be to find the right starting point.

The handoff must identify the portal’s Our Story route or linked section, specialty adaptation, copy approval, tests, exact-artifact status, deployment status, and live-verification status. The specialty portal remains primary for its subject; Smarter Justice is the broader network identity; Justice Truck remains the original and continuing community-access and field-outreach brand. Each should acknowledge the others truthfully without erasing distinct roles. Do not invent trust, reach, service-volume, outcome, law-firm, court, government, or professional-status claims.


## Public paid Human Review inheritance

Every portal handoff must reference `PUBLIC_PAID_HUMAN_REVIEW_STANDARD.md` and identify each Human Review Specialist service as adopted, narrowed, omitted, or deferred. It must preserve meaningful free AI-guided help, separate licensed-professional engagement and fees, required acknowledgments, fail-closed checkout, support/refund ownership, privacy and sensitive-data boundaries, duplicate-payment protections, and distinct code/test/exact-artifact/deployment/live/activation states. No portal may invent an incompatible checkout, fulfillment, refund, or professional-engagement model without an owner-approved documented deviation.

## Mandatory Cross-Portal Learning and Adaptation section — v1.7.28
Every complete portal continuation prompt must include a clearly headed **Cross-Portal Learning and Adaptation** section. It must identify the registry snapshot/version reviewed; exact source portal artifacts and evidence status; inbound capabilities evaluated; outbound verified lessons; decisions classified as adopted, adopt now, adapt for specialty, verify first, defer, reject, or not applicable; dependencies, tests, risks, and owner-approval needs; specialty-specific deviations; and the rule that the newest uploaded portal artifact remains implementation truth. Roadmaps and discussions are not implementation evidence. The section must prohibit blind copying and confidential public-user data centralization. The next build must update the registry after inspecting the actual ZIP.
