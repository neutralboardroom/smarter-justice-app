# Smarter Justice Seven-Practice Founding Launch Register

Version: 1.0.0  
Prepared: July 20, 2026

This is a source-controlled summary of the seven priority launch areas. It does not mark a portal live, approve confidential traffic, open payment, guarantee professional participation, or replace the separate portal codebase and continuation prompt.

## Divorce and Family Law

- **Brand:** Divorce Law Aid
- **Official domain:** divorcelawaid.com
- **Domain status:** owned
- **Public stage:** guided start available
- **Professional stage:** founding interest and profile preparation
- **Public starting page:** /divorce-family-law.html
- **Professional types:** family-law attorney; divorce attorney; mediator where permitted
- **Public workflows:**
  - Divorce and separation starting questions
  - Custody, parenting, visitation, and support organization
  - Property, debt, enforcement, and modification issue sorting
  - Adoption, guardianship, and protection-related routing
- **Professional value:**
  - Focused profile visibility after approval
  - Divorce and family-law portal participation preferences
  - Firm-seat and jurisdiction controls
  - Structured public starting information when future routing is approved
- **Important limits:**
  - State and county rules vary
  - Court deadlines and contested matters often require counsel
  - Public booking and unrestricted routing are not active
