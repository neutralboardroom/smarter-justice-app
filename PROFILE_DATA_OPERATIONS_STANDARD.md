# Smarter Justice Profile Data Operations Standard

Version: 1.0.0  
Release: v1.7.9  
Status: Exact-tested operating foundation; named owners and exercised production evidence remain pending.

## Purpose

Keep professional and firm information accurate while giving professionals, firms, organizations, and the public a controlled correction path. This standard does not create instant profile control, automatic publication, credential verification, endorsement, membership, routing, or engagement.

## Source provenance

Every public and private profile fact must retain its source type, source location or approved reference, retrieval date, supplied-by status, verification state, and later correction history. Public-source facts, professional-supplied facts, credential facts, firm-authority facts, membership facts, sponsorship facts, and staff notes must remain distinct.

## Required request types

- factual correction;
- source correction;
- duplicate merge;
- suppression from public display;
- removal review;
- professional or firm name change;
- office or contact change;
- professional departure from a firm;
- firm merger or closure;
- inactive, suspended, revoked, retired, or deceased professional;
- credential dispute;
- identity dispute;
- sponsorship or membership correction;
- suspension;
- restoration;
- appeal.

## Required workflow

1. Record the request without changing verified facts automatically.
2. Identify the requester and the authority being asserted.
3. Separate public-source facts, professional-supplied facts, staff notes, credential facts, membership facts, and sponsorship facts.
4. Review authoritative sources and preserve retrieval date and evidence.
5. Check for duplicates, aliases, prior employers, and linked firm records.
6. Apply the minimum necessary change.
7. Preserve the prior value, actor, date, reason, evidence, and decision.
8. Notify the requester through an approved channel when available.
9. Provide correction or appeal review where appropriate.
10. Reverify credentials and firm association when a material change affects eligibility.
11. Recalculate public visibility, claim control, sponsorship, and routing eligibility separately.
12. Never let payment override accuracy, credential status, suspension, removal, or public-interest safeguards.

## Urgent actions

A verified credential suspension, revocation, death, impersonation, identity theft, serious privacy exposure, or materially misleading public claim may justify immediate temporary suppression while review continues. Temporary suppression is not a final disciplinary conclusion.

## Response targets

Response targets must be approved and supported by named staff before public promises are made. The interface may state that review timing depends on request type, evidence, and operating capacity; it must not promise an unverified turnaround.

## Audit and privacy

Keep an immutable history of request, identity evidence, source evidence, decision, changes, notifications, appeal, suspension, restoration, and owner approval. Store only the minimum necessary private evidence and never publish private home addresses, identity documents, account recovery data, or private complaint details.

## Remaining gates

Named directory owner, backup owner, credential verifier, legal review, support hours, escalation, response targets, notification delivery, real-case exercise, and owner approval remain pending before broad profile promotion.
