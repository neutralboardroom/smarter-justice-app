# Smarter Justice v1.7.46 Audit Report

## Scope

Complete source audit of the exact v1.7.45 baseline across public routes, professional accounts, profiles, firms, owner/staff tools, campaign attribution, billing foundations, portal contracts, launch gates, security boundaries, tests, evidence records, and release packaging.

## Material strengths preserved

- Central professional account, verification, claim, firm, seat, revision, support, billing, and entitlement foundations.
- Free basic profile-control model separated from paid growth.
- Three-portal launch architecture and read-only handoff contract.
- Fail-closed payment, profile-publication, portal-import, opportunity, confidential-upload, and deployment gates.
- Owner authentication, MFA foundations, authorization checks, audit records, rate limiting, CSRF controls, safe redirects, and production storage safeguards.
- Extensive public UX, mobile, SEO, branding, source-provenance, profile-growth, and exact-artifact regression coverage.

## Material gaps found

1. Launch-critical owner work was fragmented across command-center, marketplace, field-launch, profile, and gate surfaces.
2. Duplicate blocker presentation made the real P0 sequence harder to operate.
3. Evidence collection lacked one launch-specific rehearsal and pause-trigger record.
4. Launch-facing public copy mixed the current legal-only, three-portal plan with historical tax/accounting, exact-price, discount, and founding-pilot claims.
5. Attorney outreach lacked one concise launch entry that explained free profile control, central management, portal selection, and paid-growth separation.
6. External acceptance remains absent for PostgreSQL, SMTP, MFA operations, monitoring, backup/restore, accessibility/device testing, exact portal D4 staging, exact rollback, and legal/commercial activation.

## Implemented v1.7.46 response

- Added private Initial Launch Activation workbench and APIs.
- Added stable plan, evidence, rehearsal, and issue records.
- Deduplicated blockers and separated P0 launch work from P1 paid growth.
- Added an attorney/law-firm launch page for the initial three portals.
- Corrected public launch messaging and removed unapproved prices and legacy active-pilot promotion.
- Added regression coverage for non-activating evidence and launch truth.

## Honest readiness conclusion

The platform is materially easier to operate and demonstrate, but it is not yet production-authorized. Source completeness does not replace external service, legal, device, accessibility, portal-staging, support, backup/restore, monitoring, or rollback evidence. Launch preflight correctly remains `NO_GO`.
