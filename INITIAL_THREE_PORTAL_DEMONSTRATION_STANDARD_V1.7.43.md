# Initial Three-Portal Demonstration Standard — v1.7.43

## Initial portals

1. Divorce Law Aid
2. Estate Law Aid
3. Personal Injury Law Aid

Each remains a separate domain, repository, artifact, deployment, brand, public directory, legal-content system, specialty-evidence authority, and builder chat.

## Demonstration story

A public user sees an exceptional specialty portal, follows a short safe starting path, and views truthful professional profiles. An attorney clicks Claim this profile, reaches Smarter Justice with verified portal context, signs in or creates one account, verifies email, submits a claim, receives staff review, manages permitted central fields, and later returns through Manage profile. Smarter Justice creates only approved, versioned, read-only portal projections.

## Personal Injury boundary

Personal Injury includes vehicle accidents and other personal-injury matters. Workers’ Compensation and Medical Malpractice remain separate. Cross-portal handoffs may explain possible overlap without combining legal pathways or profile evidence. Car Accident Law Aid remains a preserved dormant compatibility asset.

## Demonstration exclusions

Do not depend on live payments, reviews, appointments, sponsorship, real-time synchronization, two-way portal writes, confidential uploads, case-value calculators, outcome predictions, deadline guarantees, or unsupported legal conclusions.

## Quality

All surfaces must be bright-white, professional, accessible, responsive, calm, free of developer language, and complete in their visible state. Hide or disable anything unfinished.
