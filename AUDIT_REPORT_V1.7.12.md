## Complete audit finding selected for change

The platform already offered free starting-path guidance, a safe notice-description path, guided form foundations, professional profiles, professional account preparation, and fail-closed confidential storage. It did not provide a dedicated free public workspace for exact text organization or version comparison without opening sensitive server storage. That gap was material because users often need to identify what a document directly says, preserve source references, or compare revisions before deciding what help is needed.

## Implemented improvement

v1.7.12 adds a device-only document workspace that:

- reads supported local text files or pasted text inside the browser tab;
- does not transmit document contents to Smarter Justice;
- does not create an account, save the text, use AI, or share it with a professional;
- reports one-based line references and exact excerpts;
- identifies common written dates, action language, amounts, reference identifiers, contacts, likely headings, and instruction-like text;
- treats embedded instructions only as source content and never executes them;
- compares two text versions with neutral added, removed, changed, and unchanged line states;
- creates local downloadable summaries at the user’s direction;
- uses plain-language limits and does not calculate deadlines or decide legal effect.

## Known limitations

- TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON, and pasted text only; no PDF, image, OCR, handwriting, page metadata, or visual table fidelity.
- Line-oriented comparison does not reproduce formatting, signatures, exhibits, attachments, or binary document structure.
- Automated detection may miss or over-identify dates, actions, amounts, identifiers, and headings.
- A stated date is not necessarily a deadline.
- Real-browser, real-device, screen-reader, zoom, large-text, and representative-document acceptance remain external evidence items.
- All P0 launch gates remain open.
