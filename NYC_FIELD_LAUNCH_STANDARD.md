# Smarter Justice NYC Two-Sided Field Launch Standard

Version: 1.0.0  
Established: July 20, 2026

## Initial focus areas

- Downtown Brooklyn courthouse and legal-office corridor.
- Lower Manhattan courthouse and legal-office corridor.

No exact location may be described as approved until property permission, permits, courthouse-area restrictions, insurance, staffing, signage, and owner approval are verified.

## Two distinct lanes

### Public lane

- Free AI-guided starting help.
- A large “Tell us what happened” path on the user’s own device.
- Focused-portal routing.
- Optional follow-up consent.
- No confidential oral intake in public.

### Professional lane

- Review the portal network.
- Find or claim a profile.
- Create an individual or firm account.
- Select practice areas and portal interests.
- Apply for controlled founding membership.
- No guaranteed clients, inquiries, ranking, revenue, or outcomes.

## Required attribution

Every kiosk, table, building, office visit, staff member, QR code, flyer, and campaign must have a distinct code. The owner dashboard should distinguish views, scans, starts, profile searches, claim starts, applications, approvals, paid memberships, public routes, Human Review Specialist interest, office visits, and follow-ups.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Smarter Justice is not a court, government agency, bar association, law firm, tax firm, or accounting firm.
- Outreach staff provide navigation, not legal, tax, accounting, insurance, or other professional advice.
- Staff must not ask users to state confidential details where others can hear.
- No court or government affiliation may be implied.
- No lead, client, matter, revenue, eligibility, approval, or outcome may be guaranteed.
- No location opens without required permission and owner evidence.

## Field staff

Each field navigator must have a unique staff code, approved role, training status, allowed lanes, and recorded acknowledgments. Staff scripts and printed materials must use current approved public language.

## Daily operations

Record location, campaign, staff, flyers distributed, public conversations, professional conversations, office visits, follow-ups due, problems, incidents, and lessons. Do not store confidential user stories in field reports.

## Micro-portal use

Every micro-portal may create its own field assets and landing paths but must inherit campaign attribution, privacy, no-advice, no-affiliation, and no-guarantee rules from this standard.
